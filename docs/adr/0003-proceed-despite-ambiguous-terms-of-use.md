# Proceed with the sync script despite an ambiguous scouting.org Terms of Use

**Status**: accepted

#26's research found the live scouting.org Terms of Use could not be fully verified (the site 403s automated fetches), and its own clauses disagree on whether they reach `api.scouting.org` at all. The only person-level signal against third-party API use was secondhand, twice-relayed, and framed around PII/contacting youth — not aimed at a no-auth, no-PII reference endpoint.

We decided to proceed with the sync script as already scoped — manual, once a year, no PII, requirement text only — without proactively requesting written permission from `BSA.Legal@scouting.org`. This accepts residual legal/reputational risk in exchange for not blocking the MVP on an unanswerable question.

**Stop-trigger**: direct contact from BSA (email, letter, cease-and-desist) naming this site specifically. A general TOU change, policy update, or forum rumor does not trigger a stop on its own.
