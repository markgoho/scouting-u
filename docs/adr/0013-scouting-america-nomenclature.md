# Scouting America Nomenclature and Program Hierarchy

**Status**: accepted

## Context

On May 7, 2024, the Boy Scouts of America announced its official rebrand to **Scouting America** (effective February 8, 2025), reflecting the organization's mission to welcome all youth across its programs.

Throughout the site content, reference materials, guides, and documentation, clear distinctions must be maintained between the national organization and its specific youth programs.

## Decision

1. **Organization Name: Scouting America**
   - The overarching organization, national leadership, policies, advancement rules, safety protocols, and chartered bodies are referred to as **Scouting America**.
   - Use **Scouting America** when referring to national standards (*Guide to Advancement*, *Guide to Safe Scouting*, *Youth Protection*, *National Outdoor Badges*, *Scoutbook*, *Internet Advancement*).
   - Deprecate and avoid legacy "BSA" or "Boy Scouts of America" naming across site copy, titles, callouts, and diagrams (e.g. use "Scouting America Swimmer Test", "3-Pot Dishwashing System", "Scouting America Youth Protection").

2. **Program Name: Scouts BSA**
   - **Scouts BSA** is the specific program track for youth ages 11–17 operating in troops (alongside *Cub Scouting*, *Venturing*, and *Sea Scouting*).
   - Use **Scouts BSA** when referring to troop operations, patrol activities, and the specific rank progression track from Scout through Eagle.
   - The URL hierarchy remains program-first (`/scouts-bsa/...`) to allow future expansion to other Scouting America programs.

3. **Audience & Tone Standard**
   - Content is written directly for youth participants (ages 11–17, Flesch-Kincaid Grade Level 6–9), maintaining an active, second-person voice without administrative or bureaucratic jargon.
