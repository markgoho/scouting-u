First Class Rank Requirements

  1. 1a

    Since joining Scouts BSA, participate in 10 separate troop activities, at least six of which must be held outdoors.

  2. 2a

    Help plan a patrol menu for one campout that includes at least one breakfast, one lunch, and one dinner, and that requires cooking at least two of the meals.

    1. 2a(i)

      Tell how the menu includes the foods needed to cook a balanced meal.

  3. 2b

    Using the menu planned in requirement 2a, make a list showing a budget and the food amounts needed to feed three or more youth. Cook something over a fire.

  4. 3a

    Discuss when it is appropriate to use a fire for cooking versus a lightweight stove. Cook a hot meal for your patrol.

  5. Demonstrate cooking safety and cleanliness after every meal.

Note: a Scout may cook these requirements at any troop campout. See the troop cookbook for more.
