{{< drg/requirement number="6a" text_format="html" >}}
With your parent or guardian, complete the exercises in the pamphlet<i> How to Protect Your Children from Child Abuse: A Parent's Guide</i> .
{{< /drg/requirement >}}

This pamphlet comes packaged with your Scout Handbook, and it's built to be read and discussed together with a parent or guardian rather than on your own. Set aside real time for it instead of rushing through — the exercises are meant to open a conversation, not just get checked off.
