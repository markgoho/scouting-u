{{< drg/requirement number="6" text_format="html" >}}
Do the following:
{{< /drg/requirement >}}

This requirement is different from everything else in Scout rank. It isn't a skill or a piece of knowledge you're being tested on — it's about making sure you know how to keep yourself safe, and it's meant to be done together with a parent or guardian.

{{< drg/safety-first >}}
Both parts of this requirement are completed with your parent or guardian, not alone and not with your Scoutmaster. If you don't have reliable internet access at home, school, or another public place, tell your Scoutmaster — the video portion can be adjusted after talking with your parent or guardian.
{{< /drg/safety-first >}}

## What You'll Complete

- 6a. **[Complete child protection exercises](/scouts-bsa/ranks/scout/guide/req6a/)**: Work through the child-protection pamphlet's exercises together with your parent or guardian.
- 6b. **[View Personal Safety Awareness videos](/scouts-bsa/ranks/scout/guide/req6b/)**: Watch the BSA's Personal Safety Awareness videos with your parent or guardian's permission.

Together, these two exercises give you real tools, not just a rule to memorize: knowing what an unsafe situation looks like, trusting your own instinct to leave it, and knowing exactly who to go to afterward.
