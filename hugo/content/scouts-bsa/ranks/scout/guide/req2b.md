Describe the four steps of Scout advancement.
{{< /drg/requirement >}}

Every single requirement you complete, for every rank, moves through the same four steps:

1. **The Scout learns.** You're taught a skill or piece of knowledge — by an older Scout, a leader, or on your own.
2. **The Scout is tested.** You demonstrate that skill or knowledge to a qualified person, usually an older Scout or adult leader who already knows it.
3. **The Scout is reviewed.** A board of review checks that you've genuinely completed your rank's requirements and talks with you about your experience.
4. **The Scout is recognized.** You're presented with the badge in front of your troop.

Scout rank works a little differently from the ranks after it — it has no board of review. Your Scoutmaster conference (Requirement 7) covers the review step directly, and the troop then recognizes you with the badge. Starting at Tenderfoot, all four steps apply the same way, every time.
