{{< drg/requirement number="6b" text_format="html" >}}
With your parent or guardian's permission, view the <i><a href="https://www.scouting.org/training/youth-protection/scouts-bsa/"> Personal Safety Awareness videos</a></i>. (See the pamphlet inserted inside the front cover.)
{{< /drg/requirement >}}

These short videos, produced by the BSA, teach age-appropriate ways to recognize situations that aren't safe, resist and get away from them, and report them to a trusted adult. That three-part idea — recognize, resist, report — is the core message to take away, even more than any specific scene in the videos.

Watch them with your parent or guardian's permission, using the link in the requirement above.
