Rope skills are hands-on — reading about a knot only gets you so far. Have a length of rope with you as you go through these pages, and tie along as you read.

## What You'll Complete

- 4a. **[Tie 3 basic knots](/scouts-bsa/ranks/scout/guide/req4a/)**: Tie a square knot, two half-hitches, and a taut-line hitch, and know which situation each one is for.
- 4b. **[Whip and fuse rope ends](/scouts-bsa/ranks/scout/guide/req4b/)**: Seal a rope's cut end using the technique that matches its material.

Practice each knot until you can tie it without watching your own hands — that's closer to what it's actually like tying a taut-line hitch on a tent in the dark.
