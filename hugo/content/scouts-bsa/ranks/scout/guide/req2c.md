Describe the Scouts BSA ranks and how they are earned.
{{< /drg/requirement >}}

There are seven ranks in Scouts BSA, earned in this order:

1. **Scout** — the rank you're working on now.
2. **[Tenderfoot](/scouts-bsa/ranks/tenderfoot/)**
3. **[Second Class](/scouts-bsa/ranks/second-class/)**
4. **[First Class](/scouts-bsa/ranks/first-class/)**
5. **[Star](/scouts-bsa/ranks/star/)**
6. **[Life](/scouts-bsa/ranks/life/)**
7. **[Eagle](/scouts-bsa/ranks/eagle/)** — the highest rank in Scouting.

Each rank has its own list of requirements, and — just like Requirement 2b describes — every requirement is learned, tested, and reviewed before the rank is awarded. The early ranks (Scout through First Class) focus mostly on outdoor and Scoutcraft skills: knots, camping, first aid, cooking, navigation. Starting at Star, the ranks add two new ingredients that don't show up before then: a required number of merit badges and a minimum number of months of active membership between ranks. Life and Eagle raise both numbers further and add more required merit badges and, for Eagle, a large service project the Scout plans and leads.

Scout, Tenderfoot, Second Class, and First Class can technically be worked on at the same time, but they still have to be earned in that order — you can't be awarded Second Class before Tenderfoot even if you finished the requirements out of sequence.
