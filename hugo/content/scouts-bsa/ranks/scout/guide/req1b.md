{{< drg/requirement number="1b" text_format="html" >}}
Explain what Scout spirit is. Describe some ways you have shown Scout spirit by practicing the Scout Oath, Scout Law, Scout motto, and Scout slogan.
{{< /drg/requirement >}}

Scout spirit is the difference between knowing the Oath and Law and actually living by them when nobody is checking. It is not a separate thing you do — it shows up inside everything else you already do.

Your Scoutmaster is not asking you to invent new examples for this conversation. Think back over the time since you joined and pick real moments: did you help a newer Scout figure out how to pitch a tent (helpful)? Did you tell the truth about a mistake instead of hiding it (trustworthy)? Did you stay positive on a rainy campout instead of complaining (cheerful)? Two or three specific, true stories are worth far more than a list of traits with no examples behind them.

Every rank from here forward asks about Scout spirit again — this is the first of many conversations, not the last.
