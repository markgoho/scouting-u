Your patrol is your home base in the troop — a smaller group of six to eight Scouts you'll camp, cook, and compete with far more often than you'll do those things as a full troop.

## What You'll Complete

- 3a. **[Explain patrol method and types](/scouts-bsa/ranks/scout/guide/req3a/)**: Explain why Scouting organizes troops into patrols, and identify the different types your own troop uses.
- 3b. **[Explain patrol spirit](/scouts-bsa/ranks/scout/guide/req3b/)**: Learn your patrol's name, emblem, flag, and yell, and explain how they build team identity.

Find out which type of patrol you're in and who your patrol leader is before you start — both pages below assume you already know that.
