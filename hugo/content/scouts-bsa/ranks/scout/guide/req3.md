Your patrol is your home base in the troop — a smaller group of six to eight Scouts you'll camp, cook, and compete with far more often than you'll do those things as a full troop.

## Explain patrol method and types {#3a}

{{< drg/requirement number="3a" text_format="html" >}}
Explain the patrol method. Describe the types of patrols that are used in your troop.
{{< /drg/requirement >}}

The patrol method is the idea that Scouts learn best in small groups they belong to and help lead, rather than as one large troop directed entirely by adults. Each patrol elects its own patrol leader, works together at meetings and campouts, and builds its own identity.

Most troops use a few different kinds of patrols. A **new-Scout patrol** groups first-year Scouts together, often with an older Scout assigned as a guide, so newcomers can learn the ropes without feeling behind more experienced members. A **regular patrol** mixes Scouts of different ages and experience levels who stay together as they advance. Some troops also run a **Venture patrol** for older, more experienced Scouts pursuing high-adventure activities like backpacking or climbing. Find out which type your own patrol is and who your patrol leader is.

## Explain patrol spirit {#3b}

{{< drg/inherited-requirement number="3b" parent_text="" text_format="html" >}}
Become familiar with your patrol name, emblem, flag, and yell. Explain how these items create patrol spirit.
{{< /drg/inherited-requirement >}}

Every patrol picks its own name (often an animal — Wolves, Hawks, Foxes), designs an emblem to go with it, and often has its own flag and a yell it uses at competitions and campouts. None of these are required by rank requirements outside this one — patrols choose them for themselves.

These traditions matter because they turn a random assignment of Scouts into a team with its own identity. Shouting your patrol's yell before a competition, or seeing your patrol's flag flying at a campsite, builds the kind of loyalty and teamwork the patrol method depends on. If your patrol doesn't have a name, emblem, or yell yet, this requirement is a good reason to help create one.
