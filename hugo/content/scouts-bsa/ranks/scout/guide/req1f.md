{{< drg/requirement number="1f" text_format="html" >}}
Repeat from memory the Pledge of Allegiance. In your own words, explain its meaning.
{{< /drg/requirement >}}

The Pledge of Allegiance is a solemn declaration of patriotism, loyalty, and shared civic responsibility. While most Scouts have recited it in school classrooms since kindergarten, Requirement 1f asks you to understand the profound meaning behind every phrase and practice the proper flag etiquette when reciting it.

## The Text of the Pledge of Allegiance

> **I pledge allegiance to the flag of the United States of America, and to the republic for which it stands, one nation under God, indivisible, with liberty and justice for all.**

## Phrase-by-Phrase Meaning

1. **"I pledge allegiance"**
   - *Meaning*: I make a formal, personal promise of loyalty, devotion, and continuous support.
2. **"to the flag of the United States of America"**
   - *Meaning*: The American flag is the living visual emblem of our country. Honoring the flag honors our shared history, the diversity of our fifty states, and the sacrifices of veterans and citizens who built and protected our nation.
3. **"and to the republic for which it stands"**
   - *Meaning*: A **republic** is a form of government where supreme power resides with the citizens, who exercise their authority through freely elected representatives governed by the rule of law and the U.S. Constitution, rather than being ruled by a monarch or dictator.
4. **"one nation under God"**
   - *Meaning*: Fifty separate states united together as a single, sovereign nation guided by moral principles.
5. **"indivisible"**
   - *Meaning*: Our union cannot be broken, divided, or torn apart. We are bound together as one united American people.
6. **"with liberty and justice for all"**
   - *Meaning*: The core promise of American democracy—that freedom, fundamental human rights, and fair, equal protection under the law belong to every individual without exception.

## Proper Etiquette When Reciting the Pledge

- **In Full Scout Uniform**: Stand at attention, face the flag directly, and render the **Scout salute** with your right hand. Maintain the salute from the first word until the last word is completed.
- **In Civilian Clothes or Activity Uniform**: Stand at attention, remove any hat or headgear with your right hand, face the flag, and place your **right hand flat over your heart**.
- **When No Flag Is Visible**: If reciting the pledge indoors where no flag is present, turn and face toward the front of the room or the speaker.

{{< drg/tip >}}
When explaining the Pledge to your Scoutmaster, pick two phrases that mean the most to you—such as *"the republic for which it stands"* and *"with liberty and justice for all"*—and explain what they mean in your own words.
{{< /drg/tip >}}
