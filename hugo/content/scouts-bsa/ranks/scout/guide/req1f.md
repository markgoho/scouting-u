**The Pledge of Allegiance:**

> I pledge allegiance to the flag of the United States of America, and to the republic for which it stands, one nation under God, indivisible, with liberty and justice for all.

You have likely said this hundreds of times at school without stopping to unpack it, so here it is, phrase by phrase:

- **"I pledge allegiance to the flag"** — a personal promise of loyalty, made to the flag as the symbol of the country.
- **"and to the republic for which it stands"** — the promise is really to the country and its system of government, not just the piece of cloth.
- **"one nation under God"** — the country is united as a single nation.
- **"indivisible"** — that unity cannot be split apart.
- **"with liberty and justice for all"** — the promise the country makes back: freedom and fair treatment for everyone.

When you say the Pledge, stand at attention, face the flag, and give the Scout salute if you're in uniform — or place your right hand over your heart if you're not.
