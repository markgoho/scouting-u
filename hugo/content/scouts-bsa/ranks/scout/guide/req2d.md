Describe what merit badges are and how they are earned.
{{< /drg/requirement >}}

A merit badge is proof you've studied a specific subject in depth — anything from Camping to Coding to Personal Finance — and it's a completely separate track from rank requirements. There are well over a hundred merit badges available, and a Scout can start working on almost any of them right away, without waiting for a particular rank.

To earn one, you get a "blue card" from your Scoutmaster, find a registered merit badge counselor for that subject, and work through the badge's requirements one-on-one or in a small group with that counselor. The counselor signs off as you complete each requirement, and your Scoutmaster signs the card again when the badge is complete.

Merit badges connect back to rank in one place: starting at Star rank, you need a set number of merit badges — including specific "Eagle-required" ones covering things like first aid, camping, and citizenship — to advance. Nothing about Scout, Tenderfoot, Second Class, or First Class requires a merit badge, but there's nothing stopping you from starting one now.
