## Advancement Rules & Core Guidelines

{{< drg/tip >}}
**Simultaneous Work on Ranks 1–4:**  
The requirements for the **Scout, Tenderfoot, Second Class, and First Class** ranks may be worked on and signed off simultaneously. However, the ranks must be earned and conferred strictly in sequence (Scout &rarr; Tenderfoot &rarr; Second Class &rarr; First Class).
{{< /drg/tip >}}

{{< drg/did-you-know >}}
**Eagle Palms:**  
After earning the rank of Eagle Scout, a Scout can continue advancing by earning **Eagle Palms** for every 5 additional merit badges completed beyond the required 21, alongside sustained active participation and leadership in their troop.
{{< /drg/did-you-know >}}
