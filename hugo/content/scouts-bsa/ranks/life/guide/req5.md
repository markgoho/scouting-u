You already held [a position of responsibility for four months at Star](/scouts-bsa/ranks/star/guide/req5/). Life asks for the same kind of position, from the same list — but for six months this time, two months longer than Star required.

## Choosing a Position

The approved positions haven't changed from Star. They still depend on your unit type:

- **Scout troop**: patrol leader, assistant senior patrol leader, senior patrol leader, troop guide, Order of the Arrow troop representative, den chief, scribe, librarian, historian, quartermaster, bugler, junior assistant Scoutmaster, chaplain aide, instructor, webmaster, or outdoor ethics guide.
- **Venturing crew**: president, vice president, secretary, treasurer, den chief, historian, guide, quartermaster, chaplain aide, or outdoor ethics guide.
- **Sea Scout ship**: boatswain, boatswain's mate, purser, yeoman, storekeeper, crew leader, media specialist, specialist, den chief, or chaplain aide.
- **Lone Scout**: a leadership responsibility in your school, religious organization, club, or elsewhere in your community.

{{< drg/tip >}}
Assistant patrol leader still isn't an approved position for Star, Life, or Eagle, even though it's a real patrol job. Talk to your Scoutmaster before assuming a position counts.
{{< /drg/tip >}}

## A New Position, or the Same One

You can hold the same position you held at Star for another six months, or move into a new one — both are allowed. Taking on a bigger job, like moving from patrol leader to senior patrol leader, is a natural way for Life to feel like real growth rather than a repeat of Star. Whichever you choose, talk with your Scoutmaster about which positions are open before you commit.

## The Leadership Project Alternative

If no position is open, the same alternative from Star still applies: a Scoutmaster-approved leadership project that helps the troop, run for about as long and with as much real responsibility as one of the listed positions. Agree with your Scoutmaster on this before you start, not after.

## Serving Actively

"Serve actively" means the same thing it meant at Star — actually doing the job's duties for the full six months, not just holding the title. This six-month window can run alongside the six months you're logging for [requirement 1](/scouts-bsa/ranks/life/guide/req1/).

{{< drg/tip >}}
If you're moving into a new position, ask whoever held it before you what the job actually involves. Six months is a long stretch to discover partway through that the role is bigger than you expected.
{{< /drg/tip >}}
