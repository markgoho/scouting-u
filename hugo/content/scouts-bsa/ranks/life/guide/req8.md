{{< drg/requirement number="8" text_format="html" >}}
Successfully complete your board of review for the Life rank.*<br> <br> *If the board of review does not approve the Scout's advancement, the decision may be appealed in accordance with <em>Guide to Advancement</em> topic 8.0.4.0.
{{< /drg/requirement >}}

This is your fifth board of review — a short meeting with a small panel of troop adults, the same format you already know from Tenderfoot through Star.

## What the Board Confirms

The board is confirming everything Life requirement 1 through 7 asked for actually happened: six real months as an active Star Scout, six months of genuine work in your position of responsibility, real EDGE teaching that prepared another Scout, and merit badge progress toward the seven Eagle-required badges this rank asks for. Life is one rank short of Eagle, so the board may also ask how you're thinking about your Eagle Scout service project — even though that project isn't a Life requirement itself.

## What to Expect

- Be ready to talk about your position of responsibility, the Scout you taught with EDGE, and your merit badge progress.
- Questions are open-ended, aimed at an honest conversation about your growth since Star, not pass/fail trivia.
- Wear your full uniform and arrive a few minutes early, the same as every earlier board.

## If the Board Doesn't Approve

As at every earlier rank, boards of review almost always end in approval, since the requirements should already be confirmed before the board meets. If a board doesn't approve your advancement, you have the right to appeal that decision through your council, following the process in the BSA *Guide to Advancement*.

{{< drg/tip >}}
If you're not sure whether something in your record is finished — a merit badge counselor's sign-off, your conservation service split, your Scoutmaster's confirmation of your EDGE teaching — check before the board meets, not during it.
{{< /drg/tip >}}

Once the board is satisfied, you'll be notified that you've earned Life — the last rank before Eagle, and the point where most of what's left is finishing your merit badges and planning your Eagle Scout service project.
