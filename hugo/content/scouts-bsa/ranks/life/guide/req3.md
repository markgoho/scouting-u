{{< drg/requirement number="3" text_format="html" >}}
Earn five more merit badges (so that you have 11 in all) including any number more from the list for Eagle so that you have a total of seven from the required list of Eagle in that total number of 11 merit badges. You may choose any of the 17 merit badges on the required list for Eagle to fulfill this requirement. See Eagle rank requirement 3 for this list.
{{< /drg/requirement >}}

Requirement 3 advances your merit badge progression from the Star rank milestone (6 badges, at least 4 Eagle-required) to the Life rank milestone: **11 total merit badges, with at least 7 from the Eagle-required list**.

## Understanding the Advancement Mathematics

The requirement focuses on cumulative totals rather than a strict breakdown of your next five badges:

- **Total Merit Badges Needed**: 11 badges (a net increase of 5 badges from Star).
- **Eagle-Required Minimum**: At least 7 of your total 11 badges must come from the official Eagle-required list.
- **Calculating Your Remaining Eagle-Required Target**:
  - If you earned exactly 4 Eagle-required badges for Star, you must earn at least **3 more** Eagle-required badges for Life (bringing your total to 7).
  - If you earned 5 Eagle-required badges for Star, you need at least **2 more** Eagle-required badges for Life.
  - If you earned 6 Eagle-required badges for Star, you need at least **1 more** Eagle-required badge for Life.
  - Any extra badges beyond the 7 required count toward your elective total.

{{< drg/did-you-know >}}
Earning more than the required minimum of Eagle badges now puts you ahead for the Eagle Scout rank, where all 13 required categories must be completed. Nothing you earn is ever lost or wasted.
{{< /drg/did-you-know >}}

## Strategic Planning for Eagle

As a Life Scout candidate, you should strategically prioritize the merit badges that require extended tracking or multi-month commitments:

- **The 90-Day Badges**:
  - **Personal Management (Req. 2 & 8)**: Requires maintaining a budget and financial tracker for **13 consecutive weeks**.
  - **Family Life (Req. 3)**: Requires tracking personal and family responsibilities/chores for **90 continuous days**.
  - **Personal Fitness (Req. 7 & 8)**: Requires designing, logging, and completing a **12-week physical improvement program**.
- **Civic & Public Affairs Badges**:
  - **Citizenship in the Community**: Involves attending a local municipal government meeting and volunteering with a local non-profit.
  - **Citizenship in the Nation & World**: Require studying national legislation, historical documents, world treaties, and global foreign policy.
- **Elective Exploration**: Choose your optional badges to explore STEM fields, trade crafts, wilderness skills, or future career interests (e.g., *Engineering*, *Aviation*, *Woodwork*, *Electronics*, *Digital Technology*).
