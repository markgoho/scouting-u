{{< drg/requirement number="3" text_format="html" >}}
Earn five more merit badges (so that you have 11 in all) including any number more from the list for Eagle so that you have a total of seven from the required list of Eagle in that total number of 11 merit badges. You may choose any of the 17 merit badges on the required list for Eagle to fulfill this requirement. See Eagle rank requirement 3 for this list.
{{< /drg/requirement >}}

You came into Life with [6 merit badges, at least 4 of them from the Eagle-required list](/scouts-bsa/ranks/star/guide/req3/). This requirement adds five more badges, bringing your total to 11 — and it adds a second condition on top of that count: of those 11, seven have to come from the required list for Eagle.

## Doing the Math

This requirement is about your total, not a fixed split of your next five badges. Star required at least four Eagle-required badges, but Star's own requirement let you earn any of your six from that list — so you may have started Life with four, five, or all six of your badges already Eagle-required. Whatever your starting count is, subtract it from seven to find how many of your next five badges must come from the Eagle-required list. For a Scout who finished Star with exactly four, that means at least three of the next five; a Scout who already had five or six needs only two or one more. Any badges beyond that minimum can be any merit badge at all — Eagle-required or not.

- **5 more merit badges** to reach 11 total.
- **7 - (your current Eagle-required count)** of those five must come from the Eagle-required list — at least 3 for a Scout who came in with the Star minimum of 4.

## The Eagle-Required List

The same 13-badge list from Star applies here — see [Star requirement 3](/scouts-bsa/ranks/star/guide/req3/) for the full list. Any merit badge on it, including either option within a paired choice like Emergency Preparedness or Lifesaving, counts toward your seven.

## Picking Your Eagle-Required Badges

Look at which Eagle-required badges you haven't touched yet. A few things worth planning around:

- Citizenship in the Community, Citizenship in the Nation, and Citizenship in the World each take real research time and multiple counselor meetings — start whichever of these you haven't finished as early as you can.
- If you picked Camping, First Aid, or Cooking at Star, you already have the field skills for the others in that group.
- Whatever Eagle-required badges you don't finish here, you'll still need before Eagle — nothing goes to waste, so there's no harm in getting ahead.

{{< drg/tip >}}
Ask your Scoutmaster or advancement coordinator which merit badge counselors are actively working with your troop right now. A badge with a counselor ready to meet moves faster than one where you're waiting weeks for a first appointment.
{{< /drg/tip >}}

{{< drg/checklist title="Planning Your Next Five Badges" subtitle="Work through this before you approach a counselor" >}}

- Current total: count your merit badges toward the 11 this requirement needs.
- Current Eagle-required count: count how many of those are from the 13-category Eagle list, and subtract from 7 to find your remaining minimum.
- Badge choice: pick badges that fill your Eagle-required minimum first, then any others that interest you.
- Blue card: get a signed blue card from your Scoutmaster before starting each badge, not after.
{{< /drg/checklist >}}
