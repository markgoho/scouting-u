{{< drg/requirement number="1" text_format="html" >}}
Be active in your troop for at least six months as a Star Scout.
{{< /drg/requirement >}}

Star asked for four months as an active First Class Scout. Life raises that to six months as an active Star Scout — the longest single tenure clock so far, and a sign that Life expects more staying power than any rank before it.

## What Counts as Active

"Active" means the same thing it meant at [Star](/scouts-bsa/ranks/star/guide/req1/): showing up for your patrol and troop meetings and outings on a regular basis, and taking part when you're there. Missing an occasional meeting for a real reason — illness, a family trip, a school conflict — doesn't reset your clock. Your Scoutmaster confirms whether your six months counted as active, so ask early if you're unsure about an absence rather than waiting for your board of review.

## Starting the Clock

The six months start from the day you earned Star, not from when you decide to start on Life requirements. Your other Life requirements — merit badges, service hours, your position of responsibility — run on their own timelines, so you don't have to wait for this clock to finish before starting the rest.

{{< drg/tip >}}
Write down the date you earned Star, the same way you tracked your First Class date for Star. Both your Scoutmaster and your board of review will ask for it.
{{< /drg/tip >}}
