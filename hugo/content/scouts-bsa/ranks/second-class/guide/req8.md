This requirement covers two very different kinds of citizenship: showing respect for the flag, and managing your own money responsibly enough to earn, save, and spend it with a plan. The flag pieces are quick; the money pieces run over time, since you need to actually earn and track funds before you can report back on them.

## What You'll Complete

- 8a. **[Participate in flag ceremony](/scouts-bsa/ranks/second-class/guide/req8a/)**: Take part in a real flag ceremony.
- 8b. **[Explain flag etiquette](/scouts-bsa/ranks/second-class/guide/req8b/)**: Know the respect due the flag.
- 8c. **[Plan to earn money](/scouts-bsa/ranks/second-class/guide/req8c/)**: Set a savings goal and a written plan to earn it.
- 8d. **[Compare prices at 3 places](/scouts-bsa/ranks/second-class/guide/req8d/)**: Shop smart for the item you're saving for.
- 8e. **[Complete 2 hours of service](/scouts-bsa/ranks/second-class/guide/req8e/)**: Serve your community and connect it to the Scout Oath.

Start 8c as early as you can — earning money toward a goal takes real time, and 8d depends on having already completed it.
