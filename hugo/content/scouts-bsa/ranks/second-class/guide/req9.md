You watched the Personal Safety Awareness videos as a Scout. This requirement puts that training into a framework you can actually use: a simple three-word model for personal safety, and a clear answer for how to respond to bullying.

## What You'll Complete

- 9a. **[Explain 3 R's of safety](/scouts-bsa/ranks/second-class/guide/req9a/)**: Learn the three-word framework for personal safety.
- 9b. **[Describe bullying response](/scouts-bsa/ranks/second-class/guide/req9b/)**: Know what bullying is and how to respond to it.
