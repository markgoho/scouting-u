Aquatics makes its first appearance at Second Class, and it covers two very different things: proving you can handle yourself in the water, and knowing how to help someone else who can't. Plan on a supervised swim area or pool for this whole group — a certified lifeguard or aquatics instructor should be present for the swim test and rescue demonstrations.

## What You'll Complete

- 5a. **[Explain safe swim precautions](/scouts-bsa/ranks/second-class/guide/req5a/)**: Know the precautions that make a swim safe before you get in the water.
- 5b. **[Pass BSA beginner test](/scouts-bsa/ranks/second-class/guide/req5b/)**: Complete the jump, swim, turn, and return sequence.
- 5c. **[Demonstrate reaching rescues](/scouts-bsa/ranks/second-class/guide/req5c/)**: Show reaching and throwing rescue methods.
- 5d. **[Explain swimming rescue risks](/scouts-bsa/ranks/second-class/guide/req5d/)**: Understand why a swimming rescue is the last resort, not the first.

If a climate or accessibility barrier makes it genuinely impossible to complete 5b or 5c at any point in the year, talk to your Scoutmaster — councils have a process for individual exceptions, but every other part of this requirement, none of which requires entering the water, still applies.
