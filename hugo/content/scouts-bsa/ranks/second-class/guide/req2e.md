{{< drg/requirement number="2e" text_format="html" >}}
On one campout, plan and cook one hot breakfast or lunch, selecting foods from MyPlate or the current USDA nutritional model. Explain the importance of good nutrition. Demonstrate how to transport, store, and prepare the foods you selected.
{{< /drg/requirement >}}

This requirement covers the whole arc of a camp meal — planning it, hauling the ingredients in, keeping them safe to eat, and actually cooking a hot breakfast or lunch, not just eating one someone else made.

## Planning with MyPlate

<a href="https://www.myplate.gov/">MyPlate</a> divides a meal into fruits, vegetables, grains, protein, and dairy. Build your camp meal around that balance rather than reaching for whatever's easiest to pack — a hearty breakfast burrito with eggs, beans, cheese, and a piece of fruit hits more of those categories than a plain bowl of instant oatmeal.

## Why Good Nutrition Matters in Camp

A body running on junk food and sugar crashes hard on the trail — real food keeps your energy steady through a full day of hiking, swimming, or camp activities, and protein and carbohydrates together help your muscles recover overnight.

## Transporting, Storing, and Preparing

- **Transport**: pack raw meat and dairy separately from ready-to-eat food, and keep everything in a cooler or insulated bag if the hike or drive is long.
- **Store**: keep perishables cold until you cook them, and store all food away from your sleeping area to avoid attracting animals.
- **Prepare**: wash your hands before handling food, cook meat and eggs to a safe temperature, and use separate utensils for raw and cooked ingredients.

{{< drg/safety-first >}}
Undercooked eggs or meat are a common source of camp foodborne illness. Cook eggs until the whites and yolks are firm, and cook meat until it's no longer pink in the center.
{{< /drg/safety-first >}}

{{< drg/checklist title="Meal Planning" subtitle="Work through this before your campout" >}}

- Choose a breakfast or lunch that covers at least three MyPlate food groups.
- List every ingredient and how much you'll need.
- Plan how each ingredient will be transported and kept safe until cooked.
- Confirm what cooking equipment (stove, fire, cookware) the meal requires.
{{< /drg/checklist >}}
