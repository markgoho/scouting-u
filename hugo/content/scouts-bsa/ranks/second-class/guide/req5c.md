{{< drg/requirement number="5c" text_format="html" >}}
Demonstrate water rescue methods by reaching with your arm or leg, by reaching with a suitable object, and by throwing lines and objects.
{{< /drg/requirement >}}

The safest way to help a struggling swimmer is almost always the one that keeps you out of the water entirely — this requirement covers the three rescue methods you can perform from dry land or a dock, before you'd ever consider going in yourself.

## Reaching with Your Arm or Leg

If the swimmer is close to the edge, brace yourself firmly — lie down, hold onto something solid, or have someone else hold you — and extend an arm or leg for them to grab. Never lean out so far that they could pull you in.

## Reaching with a Suitable Object

When the swimmer is too far to reach with your body, extend a pole, paddle, branch, shirt, or towel toward them instead. This adds reach without putting you any closer to the water's edge.

## Throwing Lines and Objects

For a swimmer too far away to reach at all, throw a rescue rope, ring buoy, or any object that floats — a cooler, a life jacket, an empty jug — aiming just past them so the line or object lands within their reach, then pull them in once they've grabbed it.

{{< drg/safety-first >}}
"Reach, throw, don't go" is the order of priority for a reason: entering the water to rescue someone is the most dangerous option for the rescuer, and it's covered separately as its own topic in [requirement 5d](/scouts-bsa/ranks/second-class/guide/req5d/). Always try reaching and throwing methods first.
{{< /drg/safety-first >}}

{{< drg/be-prepared title="A Swimmer Struggling Near the Dock" >}}
You hear splashing and see a swimmer struggling about ten feet from the dock, too far to reach by hand.

- **Call for the lifeguard immediately** — don't try to handle it alone if trained help is nearby.
- **Grab a rescue object**: a ring buoy, pole, or anything else within reach.
- **Extend or throw it**, aiming just past the swimmer so it lands within their grasp.
- **Pull them in slowly and steadily**, keeping your own footing stable the whole time.
{{< /drg/be-prepared >}}
