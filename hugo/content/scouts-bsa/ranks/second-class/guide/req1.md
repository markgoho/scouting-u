Five activities, three outdoors, two overnights, a tent you helped pitch, the Leave No Trace principles recited from memory, and a real say in where your patrol camps — this requirement is the backbone of Second Class, and it's the one that takes the longest to finish because it lives on actual campouts, not in a single afternoon.

## What You'll Complete

- 1a. **[Participate in 5 activities](/scouts-bsa/ranks/second-class/guide/req1a/)**: Track five troop or patrol activities since joining Scouts BSA that meet the outdoor and overnight minimums.
- 1b. **[Recite Leave No Trace principles](/scouts-bsa/ranks/second-class/guide/req1b/)**: Learn the Seven Principles by heart and explain how you actually use them.
- 1c. **[Select patrol campsite](/scouts-bsa/ranks/second-class/guide/req1c/)**: Learn what makes a good patrol site and recommend one on a real campout.

Start a simple log now — date, activity, indoor or outdoor, overnight or not — so you're not trying to reconstruct five months of campouts from memory when it's time for your Scoutmaster conference.
