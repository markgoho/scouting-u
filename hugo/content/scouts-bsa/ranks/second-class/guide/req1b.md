{{< drg/requirement number="1b" text_format="html" >}}
Recite the Leave No Trace Seven Principles from memory. Explain how you follow them on all outings.
{{< /drg/requirement >}}

At Tenderfoot, you [explained how you demonstrated Leave No Trace on an outing](/scouts-bsa/ranks/tenderfoot/guide/req1c/) in general terms. This requirement asks for something more exact: the Seven Principles themselves, word for word, from memory.

## The Leave No Trace Seven Principles

1. Plan Ahead and Prepare
2. Travel and Camp on Durable Surfaces
3. Dispose of Waste Properly
4. Leave What You Find
5. Minimize Campfire Impacts
6. Respect Wildlife
7. Be Considerate of Other Visitors

These aren't just a list to memorize and forget — each one shapes a real decision you make on an outing.

## Putting Each Principle to Work

- **Plan Ahead and Prepare**: Check the weather, know the regulations for where you're going, and pack the right gear so you're not improvising in the field.
- **Travel and Camp on Durable Surfaces**: Stick to trails and established campsites instead of cutting new paths through vegetation.
- **Dispose of Waste Properly**: Pack out all trash, including food scraps, and use a cathole or established facility for human waste.
- **Leave What You Find**: Leave rocks, plants, and historical artifacts where you found them.
- **Minimize Campfire Impacts**: Use an existing fire ring when possible, keep fires small, and burn only what fully burns to ash.
- **Respect Wildlife**: Observe animals from a distance and never feed them.
- **Be Considerate of Other Visitors**: Keep noise down and yield the trail so other people can enjoy the outdoors too.

{{< drg/did-you-know >}}
A single dropped orange peel can take up to two years to decompose in the backcountry — "natural" doesn't mean fast.
{{< /drg/did-you-know >}}

## Be Ready to Explain, Not Just Recite

Reciting the seven names is only half of this requirement. For each one, be ready to describe a specific moment from a real outing where you followed it — the trail you stayed on, the trash you packed out, the fire ring you reused instead of building a new one.

{{< drg/tip >}}
Practice reciting the list in order a few times before your Scoutmaster conference. Having the order down cold makes it easier to focus on explaining each one well.
{{< /drg/tip >}}
