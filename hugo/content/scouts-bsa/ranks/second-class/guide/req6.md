At Tenderfoot, first aid meant scraped knees and bee stings. Second Class steps it up to injuries and emergencies that demand faster judgment and more serious care — rabid animal bites, heatstroke, severe bleeding, even a vehicle accident you happen to come across.

## What You'll Complete

- 6a. **[Demonstrate first aid](/scouts-bsa/ranks/second-class/guide/req6a/)**: Treat seven categories of injury and illness.
- 6b. **[Treat hurry cases](/scouts-bsa/ranks/second-class/guide/req6b/)**: Handle the emergencies that can't wait for help to arrive.
- 6c. **[Prevent outdoor injuries](/scouts-bsa/ranks/second-class/guide/req6c/)**: Know what stops these injuries before they happen.
- 6d. **[Explain emergency response](/scouts-bsa/ranks/second-class/guide/req6d/)**: Know what to do and say when you call for help.
- 6e. **[Respond to vehicle accident](/scouts-bsa/ranks/second-class/guide/req6e/)**: Know how to respond if you come across a car crash.

Work through 6a and 6b before 6c — you can't explain how to prevent an injury you haven't learned to treat yet.
