Earning money through your own labor in [Requirement 8c](/scouts-bsa/ranks/second-class/guide/req8c/) is the first half of financial literacy. Requirement 8d teaches the second essential skill: **smart consumer comparison shopping** and making deliberate decisions about how to allocate your hard-earned funds.

---

## Part 1: Comparing Costs at Three Locations

Before buying your target item, research its price across at least **three different retail sources**—such as a local specialty outfitter, a general department store, and an online merchant.

### Evaluating the "Total Cost" (Not Just the Sticker Price)
When comparing prices, look beyond the base shelf price to calculate the true final cost:
- **Sales Tax**: Local and state taxes added at checkout.
- **Shipping & Handling**: Delivery fees for online orders, or minimum order thresholds required for free shipping.
- **Quality, Warranty, and Durability**: Is the item identical across all three stores, or is one version made from lower-grade materials? Does one retailer include a lifetime replacement warranty?
- **Return Policies & Customer Support**: Can you return the item easily in person if it doesn't fit or breaks?

## Part 2: Making Your Financial Decision

Once you have completed your price comparison and your earning plan from Requirement 8c, you must decide what to do with the money:

1. **Purchase the Item as Planned**: You found the best value retailer and use your earnings to buy the item you worked for.
2. **Save All or Part of the Money**: You decide to deposit the earnings into a personal savings account, building a financial cushion or saving toward a bigger goal (such as high-adventure camp or future education).
3. **Use the Money for Another Purpose**: Your priorities shifted during the earning process, and you choose to redirect the funds toward a more urgent or meaningful need.

{{< drg/tip >}}
Any of the three choices satisfies the requirement! What matters to your Scoutmaster is that you made a conscious, well-reasoned decision rather than impulsively spending your money without thinking.
{{< /drg/tip >}}
