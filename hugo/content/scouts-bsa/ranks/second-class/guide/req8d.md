Earning the money in [requirement 8c](/scouts-bsa/ranks/second-class/guide/req8c/) was step one. This requirement is about spending it wisely — comparing real prices before you buy, and deciding what to actually do with what you earned.

## Comparing at Three Locations

Check the price for your item at a minimum of three different places — different stores, an online retailer versus a local shop, or a mix of both. Note not just the sticker price but anything that affects the real cost: tax, shipping, or a sale.

{{< drg/checklist title="Price Comparison" subtitle="Fill in as you check each location" >}}

- Location 1: price and any extra cost (tax, shipping).
- Location 2: price and any extra cost.
- Location 3: price and any extra cost.
- Which location offers the best overall value, and why.
{{< /drg/checklist >}}

## Deciding What to Do With the Money

Once you know the best price, decide what happens with what you earned in requirement 8c:

- **Use it as originally intended** — buy the item you planned for.
- **Save all or part of it** instead.
- **Use it for another purpose** you've since decided matters more.

Any of these is a legitimate outcome. Be ready to explain your reasoning — the requirement is checking that you made a deliberate choice, not that you bought exactly what you first planned to.
