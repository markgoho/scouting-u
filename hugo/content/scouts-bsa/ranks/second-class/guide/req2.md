Fire, stoves, a real meal, and two new knots — this requirement covers the tools and skills that turn a campsite into a working camp kitchen. Most of it happens on one or two campouts, so plan ahead for a trip where you can build a cooking fire and cook a real meal.

## What You'll Complete

- 2a. **[Explain when to use a fire](/scouts-bsa/ranks/second-class/guide/req2a/)**: Know when a cooking fire is the right call and when it isn't.
- 2b. **[Prepare fire materials](/scouts-bsa/ranks/second-class/guide/req2b/)**: Use a pocketknife, saw, or axe to cut tinder, kindling, and fuel wood.
- 2c. **[Build and extinguish fire](/scouts-bsa/ranks/second-class/guide/req2c/)**: Build, light, and safely put out a low-impact cooking fire.
- 2d. **[Set up camp stove](/scouts-bsa/ranks/second-class/guide/req2d/)**: Set up and safely light a lightweight or propane stove.
- 2e. **[Plan and cook a meal](/scouts-bsa/ranks/second-class/guide/req2e/)**: Plan and cook a hot breakfast or lunch using MyPlate guidance.
- 2f. **[Demonstrate sheet bend knot](/scouts-bsa/ranks/second-class/guide/req2f/)**: Tie a knot for joining two ropes of different thickness.
- 2g. **[Demonstrate bowline knot](/scouts-bsa/ranks/second-class/guide/req2g/)**: Tie a fixed loop that won't slip or jam under load.

Requirements 2b and 2c connect directly — the wood you prepare in 2b is the wood you burn in 2c — so plan to do them back to back on the same outing.
