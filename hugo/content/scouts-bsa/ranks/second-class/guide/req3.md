Everything in this requirement leads to one real hike: five miles on foot, or ten by bike, using a map and compass together. The other three pieces — reading symbols, spotting hazards, and finding direction without any tools at all — all support that one hike, so plan them around it rather than treating each as separate.

## What You'll Complete

- 3a. **[Orient map with compass](/scouts-bsa/ranks/second-class/guide/req3a/)**: Learn how a compass works and read five map symbols.
- 3b. **[Hike 5 miles](/scouts-bsa/ranks/second-class/guide/req3b/)**: Complete an approved 5-mile hike, or 10 miles by bike, using map and compass.
- 3c. **[Describe hike hazards](/scouts-bsa/ranks/second-class/guide/req3c/)**: Identify hazards or injuries you might face and how to prevent them.
- 3d. **[Find directions without compass](/scouts-bsa/ranks/second-class/guide/req3d/)**: Find direction by day and night without a compass or electronic device.

Get your adult leader's and your parent or guardian's approval for the hike route before you go — the requirement specifically calls for that approval, not just a hike you decided to take on your own.
