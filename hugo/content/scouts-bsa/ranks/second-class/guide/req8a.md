{{< drg/requirement number="8a" text_format="html" >}}
Participate in a flag ceremony for your school, religious institution, chartered organization, community, or Scouting activity.
{{< /drg/requirement >}}

You [demonstrated flag etiquette hands-on at Tenderfoot](/scouts-bsa/ranks/tenderfoot/guide/req7a/) — display, raise, lower, and fold. This requirement is about actually taking part in a real ceremony, in front of other people, not practicing alone.

## Where to Find One

- **School**: many schools open the day with a flag salute or morning assembly.
- **Religious institution**: some houses of worship include a flag ceremony at civic or patriotic events.
- **Chartered organization or community**: town events, parades, and civic ceremonies often include a color guard.
- **Scouting activity**: troop meetings frequently open or close with a flag ceremony — ask your senior patrol leader when the next one is scheduled.

## What Counts as Participating

Simply standing and saluting during someone else's ceremony can count, but look for a chance to take an active role — carrying the flag, serving on a color guard, or leading the Pledge of Allegiance — since that gives you something more concrete to describe afterward.

{{< drg/tip >}}
If your troop doesn't have a flag ceremony scheduled soon, ask your senior patrol leader to add one to an upcoming meeting. It's a common request and an easy one for the troop to accommodate.
{{< /drg/tip >}}
