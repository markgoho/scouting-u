{{< drg/requirement number="8e" text_format="html" >}}
Participate in two hours of service through one or more service projects approved by your Scoutmaster. Tell how your service to others relates to the Scout Oath.
{{< /drg/requirement >}}

You [completed one hour of service as a Tenderfoot](/scouts-bsa/ranks/tenderfoot/guide/req7b/) and connected it to the Scout slogan and motto. Second Class doubles the time to two hours and asks you to connect it to something different: the Scout Oath itself.

## Planning Your Service

Get your Scoutmaster's approval before you start — the requirement specifically requires it. You can split the two hours across more than one project if needed; it doesn't have to be a single continuous block.

## Connecting Service to the Scout Oath

The Scout Oath includes the line "to help other people at all times" — service is that promise put into action. Think about your specific project and be ready to explain how it embodied that part of the Oath, not just that service is generally a good thing.

{{< drg/tip >}}
Pick a project that connects to something you actually care about — a cause, an organization, or a need in your own community. It makes the reflection on the Scout Oath more genuine than service picked only to check the hour count.
{{< /drg/tip >}}

Be ready to describe a specific moment from your service — a task you did, a person you helped — rather than a general statement that service felt good.
