{{< drg/requirement number="5b" text_format="html" >}}
Demonstrate your ability to pass the BSA beginner test: Jump feet first into water over your head in depth, level off and swim 25 feet on the surface, stop, turn sharply, resume swimming, then return to your starting place.
{{< /drg/requirement >}}

The BSA beginner test isn't about swimming style or speed — it's a straightforward check that you can enter deep water under control, keep swimming, and get yourself back, all without panicking.

## The Sequence

1. **Jump feet first** into water over your head in depth.
2. **Level off** and begin swimming on the surface.
3. **Swim 25 feet.**
4. **Stop, turn sharply**, and change direction.
5. **Resume swimming.**
6. **Return to your starting place.**

This must be done under the supervision of a lifeguard or certified aquatics instructor, as part of the Safe Swim Defense precautions from [requirement 5a](/scouts-bsa/ranks/second-class/guide/req5a/).

## What the Test Is Actually Checking

Each part of the sequence targets a specific skill: the jump proves you can enter deep water calmly, leveling off shows you can transition from jump to swim without swallowing water or floundering, the sharp turn shows control and the ability to change direction mid-swim, and the return leg proves you can sustain the swim rather than just complete a single length.

{{< drg/tip >}}
If you haven't taken a formal swim test recently, practice each piece separately first — jumping into deep water, then a controlled 25-foot swim, then a sharp turn — before stringing the whole sequence together.
{{< /drg/tip >}}
