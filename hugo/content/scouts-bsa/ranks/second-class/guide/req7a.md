{{< drg/requirement number="7a" text_format="html" >}}
After completing Tenderfoot requirement 6c, be physically active at least 30 minutes each day for five days a week for four weeks. Keep track of your activities.
{{< /drg/requirement >}}

Your Tenderfoot fitness plan ran 30 days total. This one asks for a full four weeks of consistency — at least 30 minutes of activity, five days each week, every week — which is a longer stretch of steady habit-building than a single 30-day sprint.

## The Standard

- **30 minutes minimum** per active day — running, biking, swimming, sports practice, or any activity that gets your heart rate up counts.
- **Five days a week**, not necessarily the same five days every week.
- **Four full weeks in a row.**

This must happen *after* [Tenderfoot requirement 6c](/scouts-bsa/ranks/tenderfoot/guide/req6c/) is complete — confirm that before you start counting days here.

## Keeping Track

Log the date, activity, and duration each day you're active. A simple daily list is enough — the point is having real data to look back on for [requirement 7b](/scouts-bsa/ranks/second-class/guide/req7b/), not a polished record.

{{< drg/checklist title="4-Week Activity Log" subtitle="Track each active day" >}}

- Week 1: five active days logged, each 30+ minutes.
- Week 2: five active days logged, each 30+ minutes.
- Week 3: five active days logged, each 30+ minutes.
- Week 4: five active days logged, each 30+ minutes.
{{< /drg/checklist >}}

{{< drg/tip >}}
Pick activities you'd actually choose to do — a sport, a bike ride with friends, a hike — rather than forcing the same routine every day. Four weeks is much easier to finish when it doesn't feel like a chore.
{{< /drg/tip >}}
