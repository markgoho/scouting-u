{{< drg/requirement number="10" text_format="html" >}}
Demonstrate Scout spirit by living the Scout Oath and Scout Law. Tell how you have done your duty to God and how you have lived four different points of the Scout Law (not to include those used for Tenderfoot requirement 9) in your everyday life.
{{< /drg/requirement >}}

You [demonstrated Scout spirit through four points of the Law at Tenderfoot](/scouts-bsa/ranks/tenderfoot/guide/req9/). This requirement asks for four *different* points — new examples from new points, not a repeat of what you already reported.

## Duty to God

As before, this part is personal and tied to your own faith tradition or beliefs. Come with a specific, concrete example — something you actually did or observed — rather than a general statement.

## Four New Points of the Law

The Scout Law has twelve points: trustworthy, loyal, helpful, friendly, courteous, kind, obedient, cheerful, thrifty, brave, clean, and reverent. Whichever four you used for Tenderfoot are off-limits here — pick four different ones and think of a real, recent example for each.

{{< drg/tip >}}
Keep a running note of which points you've already used at each rank. Scout spirit repeats at nearly every rank ahead of you, and you'll need fresh points and fresh examples again at Star, Life, and beyond.
{{< /drg/tip >}}

This requirement is graded on whether your examples are real, specific, and drawn from your actual life since your last rank — not on which four points you happen to choose.
