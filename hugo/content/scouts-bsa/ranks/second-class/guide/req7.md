You measured and improved your fitness for 30 days at Tenderfoot. Second Class turns that into a habit — four full weeks of regular activity, a real plan for keeping it going, and an honest look at drugs, alcohol, and tobacco.

## What You'll Complete

- 7a. **[Be physically active 4 weeks](/scouts-bsa/ranks/second-class/guide/req7a/)**: Stay active at least 30 minutes a day, five days a week, for four weeks.
- 7b. **[Set a fitness goal](/scouts-bsa/ranks/second-class/guide/req7b/)**: Reflect on 7a and set a goal for staying active going forward.
- 7c. **[Learn substance abuse dangers](/scouts-bsa/ranks/second-class/guide/req7c/)**: Participate in a program on drugs, alcohol, and tobacco and connect it to Scout values.

Requirement 7a must come after [Tenderfoot requirement 6c](/scouts-bsa/ranks/tenderfoot/guide/req6c/) — confirm that's done before you start tracking these four weeks.
