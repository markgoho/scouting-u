{{< drg/requirement number="7b" text_format="html" >}}
Share your challenges and successes in completing Second Class requirement 7a. Set a goal for continuing to include physical activity as part of your daily life and develop a plan for doing so.
{{< /drg/requirement >}}

Four weeks of activity logs from [requirement 7a](/scouts-bsa/ranks/second-class/guide/req7a/) are only half the point — this requirement asks what you actually learned from it, and what you're going to do with that going forward.

## Reflecting on Challenges and Successes

Look back at your log honestly. Which days were easy to stay active, and which ones took real effort? Did a particular activity keep you motivated, or did you struggle with the same excuse more than once? Be ready to share this with your Scoutmaster — specific, honest reflection matters more than a polished summary.

## Setting a Real Goal

A useful goal is specific and realistic, not vague. "Be more active" isn't a plan. "Bike to school three days a week" or "join a spring sport" is.

## Building the Plan

- **What** you'll do to stay active.
- **How often**, in concrete terms.
- **What might get in the way**, and how you'll handle it — bad weather, a busy week, low motivation.

Keep your own notes on this goal and plan rather than filling in a fillable form — the plan means more when you've actually written it in your own words.

{{< drg/tip >}}
Pick a goal you'd keep even without a rank requirement attached to it. The four weeks from 7a proved you can build a habit — this requirement is about making sure the habit outlasts the requirement.
{{< /drg/tip >}}
