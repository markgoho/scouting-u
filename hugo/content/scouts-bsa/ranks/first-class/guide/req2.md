At Second Class, you planned and cooked one meal. First Class takes you through the whole job of running a patrol's kitchen for a full campout — planning three meals, budgeting for them, gathering the right gear, handling food safely, and finally running the cook duty yourself while supervising an assistant.

## What You'll Complete

- 2a. **[Plan campout menu](/scouts-bsa/ranks/first-class/guide/req2a/)**: Plan a full breakfast, lunch, and dinner menu that meets MyPlate nutrition guidance.
- 2b. **[Develop menu budget](/scouts-bsa/ranks/first-class/guide/req2b/)**: Turn that menu into a shopping list with quantities and a budget for three or more youth, then secure the ingredients.
- 2c. **[Show cooking gear needed](/scouts-bsa/ranks/first-class/guide/req2c/)**: Identify every pan, utensil, and piece of gear the menu requires.
- 2d. **[Handle food safely](/scouts-bsa/ranks/first-class/guide/req2d/)**: Demonstrate safe handling and storage of perishable food, and proper disposal of camp garbage and waste water.
- 2e. **[Serve as campout cook](/scouts-bsa/ranks/first-class/guide/req2e/)**: On a real campout, cook all three meals from 2a and supervise an assistant and the cleanup.

These five requirements build on each other in order — the menu from 2a drives the budget in 2b, the gear list in 2c, and the actual cooking in 2e — so it's worth completing all five around the same campout rather than spreading them across unrelated trips.
