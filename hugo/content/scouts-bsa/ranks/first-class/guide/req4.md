## What You'll Complete

- 4a. **[Complete orienteering course](/scouts-bsa/ranks/first-class/guide/req4a/)**: Run a real map-and-compass course over at least a mile and measure something too tall or too wide to pace off directly.
- 4b. **[Navigate with GPS](/scouts-bsa/ranks/first-class/guide/req4b/)**: Plan and follow a route to a destination using a GPS unit or app on a campout or hike.

You oriented a map with a compass for Second Class. This requirement asks you to put that skill to work over real distance and add a second, modern layer: electronic navigation. Do 4a first — the pacing and sighting skills you sharpen there make the GPS work in 4b easier to understand, because you'll know what the device is doing instead of just following an arrow on a screen.
