This requirement pulls together the first-aid and emergency skills you've built at every rank so far and pushes them toward real independence — not just treating an injury in front of you, but planning ahead for emergencies at home before they happen.

## What You'll Complete

- 7a. **[Apply bandages](/scouts-bsa/ranks/first-class/guide/req7a/)**: Bandage a sprained ankle and injuries to the head, upper arm, and collarbone.
- 7b. **[Transport an injured person](/scouts-bsa/ranks/first-class/guide/req7b/)**: Move an injured person alone and with a partner, two different ways.
- 7c. **[Explain heart attack and CPR](/scouts-bsa/ranks/first-class/guide/req7c/)**: Recognize a heart attack and explain the steps of CPR.
- 7d. **[Describe utility hazards](/scouts-bsa/ranks/first-class/guide/req7d/)**: Identify the utilities at your home or meeting place and their hazards.
- 7e. **[Develop home emergency plan](/scouts-bsa/ranks/first-class/guide/req7e/)**: Build a plan for fire, storm, power outage, and water outage.
- 7f. **[Explain emergency water sourcing](/scouts-bsa/ranks/first-class/guide/req7f/)**: Know how to find safe drinking water in an emergency.

The first three pages (7a-7c) are hands-on skills you'll demonstrate physically. The last three (7d-7f) shift to planning — thinking through your own home before an emergency forces you to think on the spot.
