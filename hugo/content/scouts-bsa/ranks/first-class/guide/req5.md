## What You'll Complete

- 5a. **[Identify 10 native plants](/scouts-bsa/ranks/first-class/guide/req5a/)**: Identify or show evidence of at least 10 native plant species from your own area.
- 5b. **[Obtain weather forecast](/scouts-bsa/ranks/first-class/guide/req5b/)**: Name two ways to get a weather forecast and explain why checking one matters before you plan an activity.
- 5c. **[Describe hazardous weather signs](/scouts-bsa/ranks/first-class/guide/req5c/)**: Describe at least three natural warning signs of dangerous weather, what they can lead to, and how to respond.
- 5d. **[Describe local extreme weather](/scouts-bsa/ranks/first-class/guide/req5d/)**: Describe the extreme weather your own area actually produces, and how you'd plan around it.

This requirement covers two different skills that happen to share a group heading: knowing your local plant life, and reading the sky. 5a stands alone — it's a field-identification task you can do on any hike. 5b through 5d build on each other: first learn where forecasts come from, then what warning signs look like in the moment, then how your specific region's weather risks should shape your planning.
