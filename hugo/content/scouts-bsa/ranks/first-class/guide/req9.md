This requirement is the broadest of the four foundational ranks' citizenship work — it moves from your community's rights and issues into your own habits, and closes with hands-on service.

## What You'll Complete

- 9a. **[Discuss citizen rights and duties](/scouts-bsa/ranks/first-class/guide/req9a/)**: Talk with an approved community leader about the constitutional rights and obligations of a U.S. citizen.
- 9b. **[Investigate environmental issue](/scouts-bsa/ranks/first-class/guide/req9b/)**: Research a real environmental issue in your community and share what you find.
- 9c. **[Reduce outing waste](/scouts-bsa/ranks/first-class/guide/req9c/)**: Measure your own waste on an outing, then act to reduce it on the next one.
- 9d. **[Complete 3 hours of service](/scouts-bsa/ranks/first-class/guide/req9d/)**: Complete a new service project and connect it to the Scout Law.

These four don't have to happen in a strict sequence, but 9c takes two separate outings to complete, so start it early rather than leaving it for last.
