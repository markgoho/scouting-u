{{< drg/requirement number="12" text_format="html" >}}
While working toward the First Class rank, and after completing Second Class requirement 11, participate in a Scoutmaster conference.
{{< /drg/requirement >}}

You've now sat through [a Scoutmaster conference at Tenderfoot](/scouts-bsa/ranks/tenderfoot/guide/req10/) and [another at Second Class](/scouts-bsa/ranks/second-class/guide/req11/), so the format is familiar: a one-on-one conversation, not a test. What's different this time is the content you're bringing to it.

## What's Different at First Class

This is the biggest rank yet — thirteen requirements covering camping, cooking, tools, navigation, nature, aquatics, first aid, fitness, and citizenship, on top of Scout spirit. Expect your Scoutmaster to draw on all of it: the campouts you actually camped on, the orienteering course you actually ran, the emergency plan you actually wrote for your own home.

## Getting Ready

- Review each requirement and be ready to talk about what you actually did, not just what the requirement says.
- Bring your activity log from [requirement 1a](/scouts-bsa/ranks/first-class/guide/req1a/) and your fitness log from [requirement 8a](/scouts-bsa/ranks/first-class/guide/req8a/).
- Think through your Scout spirit examples from [requirement 11](/scouts-bsa/ranks/first-class/guide/req11/) ahead of time.

{{< drg/tip >}}
This requirement specifically comes after Second Class requirement 11 — confirm that conference is complete before scheduling this one.
{{< /drg/tip >}}
