{{< drg/requirement number="9d" text_format="html" >}}
Participate in three hours of service through one or more service projects approved by your Scoutmaster. The project(s) must not be the same service project(s) used for Tenderfoot requirement 7b and Second Class requirement 8e. Explain how your service to others relates to the Scout Law.
{{< /drg/requirement >}}

You've now done service at every rank: [one hour at Tenderfoot](/scouts-bsa/ranks/tenderfoot/guide/req7b/) tied to the Scout slogan and motto, and [two hours at Second Class](/scouts-bsa/ranks/second-class/guide/req8e/) tied to the Scout Oath. First Class raises the time to three hours and asks for a different connection: the Scout Law itself.

## Planning a New Project

Get your Scoutmaster's approval before you start. The project — or projects, if you split the hours — must be new: it cannot be the same project you already used for Tenderfoot requirement 7b or Second Class requirement 8e. Pick something new, even if it's with the same organization as before.

## Connecting Service to the Scout Law

The Scout Law names twelve points: trustworthy, loyal, helpful, friendly, courteous, kind, obedient, cheerful, thrifty, brave, clean, and reverent. Service work usually touches several of these at once — think about which ones showed up in what you actually did:

- **Helpful** and **kind** show up directly in almost any service project.
- **Trustworthy** might show up in following through on a commitment you made to an organization.
- **Cheerful** might show up in how you handled a task that wasn't fun.

Pick the point or points that genuinely connect to your project — don't force a connection that isn't really there.

{{< drg/tip >}}
Take a few notes right after your service hours while the details are fresh — what you did, who you worked with, a specific moment. It's much easier to explain a real connection to the Scout Law from fresh notes than from memory weeks later.
{{< /drg/tip >}}

Be ready to describe a specific moment from your three hours — not just that service felt good, but how that moment connects to a point of the Law you can name.
