{{< drg/requirement number="9c" text_format="html" >}}
On a Scouting or family outing, take note of the trash and garbage you produce. Before your next similar outing, decide how you can reduce, recycle, or repurpose what you take on that outing, and then put those plans into action. Compare your results.
{{< /drg/requirement >}}

This requirement is a real before-and-after experiment, and it needs two separate outings to complete — you can't do it in a single trip.

## Outing One: Measure

On a Scouting or family outing, pay attention to the trash and garbage your group actually produces — packaging, food waste, single-use items. Keep a simple count or description of what ended up in the trash by the end of the outing.

## Between Outings: Plan

Before your next similar outing, decide specific changes based on what you noticed. Think in terms of the three verbs the requirement names:

- **Reduce**: bring less packaging in the first place — bulk food instead of individually wrapped snacks, reusable containers instead of disposable ones.
- **Recycle**: sort out anything recyclable instead of sending it all to the trash.
- **Repurpose**: reuse containers, bags, or gear instead of buying single-use versions.

## Outing Two: Act and Compare

Put your plan into action on the next similar outing, then compare your results to the first one. Did you actually produce less trash? What worked, and what didn't go as planned?

{{< drg/tip >}}
"Similar outing" matters — comparing a weekend campout to a single afternoon hike won't give you an honest before-and-after. Pick two outings close enough in type and length that the comparison actually means something.
{{< /drg/tip >}}

This connects directly back to [Leave No Trace](/scouts-bsa/ranks/first-class/guide/req1b/), which you already explained for requirement 1b — this requirement puts that principle into practice with your own measured results, not just an explanation of why it matters.
