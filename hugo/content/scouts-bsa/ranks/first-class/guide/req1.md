Ten activities, six outdoors, three overnights — the same shape as Second Class 1a, just bigger numbers — plus a step up in how you talk about the outdoors: instead of reciting Leave No Trace, you now explain *why* it exists and what happens without it.

## What You'll Complete

- 1a. **[Participate in 10 activities](/scouts-bsa/ranks/first-class/guide/req1a/)**: Log 10 troop or patrol activities since joining Scouts BSA, with outdoor and overnight minimums built in.
- 1b. **[Explain camping impacts](/scouts-bsa/ranks/first-class/guide/req1b/)**: Explain what camping actually does to the environment and to other outdoor users, and why the Outdoor Code and Leave No Trace exist to protect against it.

Keep counting activities from the moment you joined Scouts BSA, not from when you started First Class — activities you already logged for Tenderfoot and Second Class still count here.
