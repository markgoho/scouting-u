{{< drg/requirement number="11" text_format="html" >}}
Demonstrate Scout spirit by living the Scout Oath and Scout Law. Tell how you have done your duty to God and how you have lived four different points of the Scout Law (different from those points used for previous ranks) in your everyday life.
{{< /drg/requirement >}}

You've now demonstrated Scout spirit through four points of the Law at both [Tenderfoot](/scouts-bsa/ranks/tenderfoot/guide/req9/) and [Second Class](/scouts-bsa/ranks/second-class/guide/req10/). This requirement is stricter than either of those: the four points you use here must be different from every point used at *any* previous rank, not just the one right before it.

## Duty to God

As before, this part is personal and tied to your own faith tradition or beliefs. Come with a specific, concrete example — something you actually did or observed — rather than a general statement.

## Four New Points of the Law

The Scout Law has twelve points: trustworthy, loyal, helpful, friendly, courteous, kind, obedient, cheerful, thrifty, brave, clean, and reverent. Whichever eight you've already used across Tenderfoot and Second Class are off-limits — pick four different ones from what's left and think of a real, recent example for each.

{{< drg/did-you-know >}}
If you used four different points at Tenderfoot and four more at Second Class, you've already used eight of the Law's twelve points — which means this requirement doesn't leave you a choice. The four points left over are the exact four you have to use here.
{{< /drg/did-you-know >}}

{{< drg/tip >}}
Check your notes from Tenderfoot and Second Class before you pick your four points here. If you didn't write them down at the time, ask your Scoutmaster — your earlier Scoutmaster conferences and boards of review are on record with what you reported.
{{< /drg/tip >}}

This requirement is graded on whether your examples are real, specific, and drawn from your actual life since your last rank — not on which four points you happen to have left.
