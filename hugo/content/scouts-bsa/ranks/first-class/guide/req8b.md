Share your challenges and successes in completing First Class requirement 8a. Set a goal for continuing to include physical activity as part of your daily life.
{{< /drg/requirement >}}

Your four-week log from [requirement 8a](/scouts-bsa/ranks/first-class/guide/req8a/) is the raw material — this requirement asks what you actually learned from running the cycle a second time.

## Share Your Challenges and Successes

Look back at your log honestly. What got in the way some days — schedule, weather, motivation — and how did you push through, or not? What worked better this time than it did during your Second Class cycle? Be ready to talk through this with your Scoutmaster with real specifics from your own log, not a general statement that "it went fine."

## Set a Goal

This requirement asks you to set a goal for keeping physical activity part of your daily life — it doesn't ask you to write out a full plan the way Second Class 7b did. A useful goal is still specific rather than vague: "be more active" isn't a goal, but "walk or bike to school three days a week" is.

{{< drg/tip >}}
Pick a goal you'd keep even without a rank requirement attached to it. Two four-week cycles have already shown you can build the habit — this is about naming what keeps it going once the logging stops.
{{< /drg/tip >}}
