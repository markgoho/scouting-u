{{< drg/requirement number="2e" text_format="html" >}}
On one campout, serve as cook. Supervise your assistant(s) in using a stove or building a cooking fire. Prepare the breakfast, lunch, and dinner planned in First Class requirement 2a. Supervise the cleanup.
{{< /drg/requirement >}}

Every piece you've built in this requirement group — the [menu](/scouts-bsa/ranks/first-class/guide/req2a/), the [budget and ingredients](/scouts-bsa/ranks/first-class/guide/req2b/), the [gear](/scouts-bsa/ranks/first-class/guide/req2c/), and the [safe-handling procedures](/scouts-bsa/ranks/first-class/guide/req2d/) — comes together here. On one campout, you are the cook. That's a different job than the meal you cooked at Second Class: this time you're also supervising an assistant, not just cooking solo.

## What the Cook Does

- **Prepare all three meals** from your requirement 2a menu: breakfast, lunch, and dinner, cooking whichever of them your plan called for.
- **Supervise your assistant(s)** in using a stove or building a cooking fire — you're directing that work, not necessarily doing every step yourself.
- **Supervise the cleanup** after each meal, making sure gear gets washed, waste is handled the way you planned in requirement 2d, and the cook site is left clean.

## Supervising, Not Just Doing

Supervising means giving your assistant real, specific direction — what to light, how much wood or fuel to use, when to add ingredients — and checking their work as they go, the same way an adult leader would check yours. If your assistant has never lit a stove or built a fire, walk them through it step by step rather than taking over and doing it yourself.

{{< drg/safety-first >}}
Whether you're using a stove or a cooking fire, confirm your assistant knows the safety basics before they start: never leave a flame unattended, keep a clear area around it, and know how to shut off fuel or extinguish the fire completely.
{{< /drg/safety-first >}}

## Running the Full Day

Pace yourself across all three meals — breakfast prep often starts before most of camp is awake, and dinner cleanup can run late. Delegate tasks across your meal shifts so the same person isn't doing every job alone, and use the checklist below to keep the day organized.

{{< drg/checklist title="Cook Duty" subtitle="Confirm before, during, and after each meal" >}}

- Ingredients and gear staged and ready before cooking starts.
- Assistant briefed on the stove or fire task before they begin.
- Meal cooked to a safe temperature and served.
- Cleanup supervised: gear washed, waste handled, site left clean.
{{< /drg/checklist >}}
