You can't measure improvement without a starting point. This first test isn't about hitting a target number — there isn't one — it's about honestly recording where you are today so requirement 6c has something real to compare against.

## Running the Tests

- **Push-ups**: as many correct-form push-ups as you can do in 60 seconds.
- **Sit-ups or curl-ups**: as many as you can do with correct form in 60 seconds.
- **Back-saver sit-and-reach**: how far you can stretch toward (or past) your toes while seated, one leg extended.

{{< drg/image src="images/sit-and-reach-test-position.png" alt="Diagram showing the correct seated position for the back-saver sit-and-reach fitness test, one leg extended, reaching forward" >}}
- **1-mile walk/run**: your time to cover one mile.

## Doing It Right

- Warm up first — a few minutes of light movement reduces injury risk and gives you a more honest result.
- Use correct form on push-ups and sit-ups; a sloppy rep that "doesn't count" toward form standards doesn't reflect your real fitness.
- Record your actual numbers, even if they're not what you hoped for. This is a baseline, not a grade — an honest low number gives you real room to show improvement in 6c.

{{< drg/tip >}}
Have a buddy or leader watch and count for you. It's hard to keep accurate form and an accurate count at the same time when you're pushing yourself.
{{< /drg/tip >}}
