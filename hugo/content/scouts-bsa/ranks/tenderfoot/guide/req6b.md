{{< drg/requirement number="6b" text_format="html" >}}
Develop and describe a plan for improvement in each of the activities listed in Tenderfoot requirement 6a. Keep track of your activity for at least 30 days.
{{< /drg/requirement >}}

A number from requirement 6a means nothing without a plan to move it. This requirement is where you decide what you're actually going to do about your baseline — and then prove you did it by tracking at least 30 days of real activity.

## Building Your Plan

For each of the four tests, pick a specific, realistic activity:

- **Push-ups**: a set number of push-ups, a few times a week, gradually increasing.
- **Sit-ups/curl-ups**: same approach — start where you are, add repetitions over time.
- **Sit-and-reach (flexibility)**: a short daily stretching routine.
- **1-mile walk/run**: regular runs, walks, or interval training to build endurance.

Keep it realistic. A plan that demands an hour a day, every day, usually fails within a week — a plan you can actually stick to for at least 30 days beats an ambitious one you abandon after three.

## Tracking at Least 30 Days

Keep a log with real dates and real entries — what you actually did each day or week, not just what you planned. The requirement asks you to *keep track of your activity for at least 30 days*, so more than 30 days of tracking is fine, but fewer isn't — a log you can point to matters more than a plan you never followed.

{{< drg/tip >}}
Set a specific day and time for each activity — "Tuesday and Thursday after school" beats "whenever I have time." Vague plans are the easiest ones to skip.
{{< /drg/tip >}}
