Meals on a campout are a team job, not room service. This requirement covers the three parts of that job: helping cook, cleaning up safely afterward, and understanding why the patrol eats together instead of everyone fending for themselves.

## What You'll Complete

- 2a. **[Help cook a meal](/scouts-bsa/ranks/tenderfoot/guide/req2a/)**: Assist with preparing one patrol meal and explain why everyone shares the work.
- 2b. **[Clean cooking and eating gear](/scouts-bsa/ranks/tenderfoot/guide/req2b/)**: Demonstrate a safe way to clean cookware and dishes in the field.
- 2c. **[Explain patrol meal importance](/scouts-bsa/ranks/tenderfoot/guide/req2c/)**: Explain why the patrol eats meals together.

Plan to complete all three on the same campout you use for requirement 1 — cooking, cleanup, and the patrol meal all happen naturally on any overnight outing.
