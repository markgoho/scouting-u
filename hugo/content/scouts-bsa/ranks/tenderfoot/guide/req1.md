This requirement is really one overnight campout, broken into three things you'll show while you're there: that you packed right, that you can pitch and sleep in a tent, and that you left the site the way you found it.

## What You'll Complete

- 1a. **[Show and pack camping gear](/scouts-bsa/ranks/tenderfoot/guide/req1a/)**: Show your leader the gear you're bringing and pack it the right way.
- 1b. **[Sleep in a tent you pitch](/scouts-bsa/ranks/tenderfoot/guide/req1b/)**: Pitch a tent yourself and spend at least one night in it.
- 1c. **[Explain outdoor ethics](/scouts-bsa/ranks/tenderfoot/guide/req1c/)**: Explain how you followed the Outdoor Code and Leave No Trace on your outings.

All three happen on the same trip, so read through this whole requirement before you leave — you'll want to plan your packing and your outdoor-ethics observations together, not scramble to remember them afterward.
