{{< drg/requirement number="3d" text_format="html" >}}
Demonstrate proper care, sharpening, and use of the knife, saw, and ax. Describe when each should be used.
{{< /drg/requirement >}}

You covered [basic pocketknife safety](/scouts-bsa/ranks/scout/guide/req5/) as a Scout. Tenderfoot expands that into the full set of edged tools you'll use throughout Scouting — knife, saw, and ax — and adds a skill Scout rank didn't cover: keeping them sharp.

## Picking the Right Tool

| Tool | Best for | Not for |
|---|---|---|
| Knife | Fine work: whittling, food prep, cutting cord | Chopping wood, splitting logs |
| Saw | Cutting branches or logs to length | Fine carving, splitting |
| Ax | Splitting and limbing larger wood | Fine work, anything near your body |

A common mistake is reaching for the biggest tool for a small job. Match the tool to the task — most camp jobs only need a knife.

## Care and Sharpening

- **Clean and dry** each tool after use, especially a saw blade, which rusts fast.
- **Oil moving parts** — a folding knife's pivot, a folding saw's hinge.
- **Sharpen with a whetstone or sharpening puck**, drawing the edge across at a consistent angle (usually 15-20 degrees for a knife). A dull blade is more dangerous than a sharp one — it slips instead of cutting, and slipping is how most cuts happen.

{{< drg/image src="images/knife-sharpening-angle.png" alt="Diagram showing a knife blade held at the correct 15-20 degree angle against a whetstone during sharpening" >}}
- **Store with the edge protected** — a sheath for a knife or ax, a folded or sheathed blade for a saw.

{{< drg/safety-first >}}
Always cut away from your body, keep your "blood circle" — an arm's length in every direction — clear of other people, and never pass an open knife, saw, or ax to someone else; close or sheathe it first and hand it over closed.
{{< /drg/safety-first >}}

{{< drg/image src="images/blood-circle-safety-zone.png" alt="Spatial diagram of a Scout using a knife, with an arm's-length blood circle clearance radius marked around them" >}}

{{< drg/be-prepared title="Your Knife Slips While Whittling" >}}
A dull or misused blade can slip and cause a cut even when you're being careful.

- **Step one**: Stop immediately and check the wound. Apply direct pressure with a clean cloth or gauze.
- **Step two**: For a minor cut, clean it and apply a bandage from your first-aid kit (see requirement 4d).
- **Step three**: For a deep cut that won't stop bleeding, keep pressure on and get an adult leader immediately.
{{< /drg/be-prepared >}}

Your troop or council likely requires a Totin' Chip before you can carry and use a knife, ax, or saw on your own — ask your Scoutmaster if you haven't earned yours yet.
