Hiking looks simple — walk somewhere, walk back — but the safety and ethics behind it take real thought. This requirement covers four pieces: staying paired up, knowing what to do if that pairing breaks down, following the rules of the road and trail, and protecting the ground you walk on.

## What You'll Complete

- 5a. **[Use buddy system](/scouts-bsa/ranks/tenderfoot/guide/req5a/)**: Explain why the buddy system matters and use it on an actual outing.
- 5b. **[Explain what to do if lost](/scouts-bsa/ranks/tenderfoot/guide/req5b/)**: Describe the steps to take if you get separated or lost.
- 5c. **[Explain safe hiking rules](/scouts-bsa/ranks/tenderfoot/guide/req5c/)**: Explain safe hiking rules for roads and cross-country, day and night.
- 5d. **[Explain hiking on durable surfaces](/scouts-bsa/ranks/tenderfoot/guide/req5d/)**: Explain why durable surfaces matter and identify some from your own hike.

These four build on each other: the buddy system and knowing what to do if lost are both about staying safe *with people*, while safe hiking rules and durable surfaces are about moving through the terrain itself.
