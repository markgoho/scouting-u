{{< drg/requirement number="10" text_format="html" >}}
While working toward the Tenderfoot rank, and after completing Scout rank requirement 7, participate in a Scoutmaster conference.
{{< /drg/requirement >}}

You [already sat through a Scoutmaster conference](/scouts-bsa/ranks/scout/guide/req7/) to earn Scout rank, so the format won't surprise you: a one-on-one conversation with your Scoutmaster, not a test. What's different this time is the content — you're bringing a full rank's worth of new skills instead of just joining the troop.

## What's Different at Tenderfoot

Where your Scout-rank conference mostly checked that you understood the basics of the program, this one reviews everything you did across all eleven Tenderfoot requirements: camping, cooking, knots, first aid, hiking, fitness, citizenship, and Scout spirit. Expect your Scoutmaster to ask about specific moments from your campouts, not just whether boxes got checked.

## Getting Ready

- Review each requirement and be ready to talk about what you actually did, not just what the requirement says.
- Bring any notes or worksheets you completed — your fitness plan, your first-aid kit list.
- Be honest about anything you're still unsure of. This conversation is meant to catch gaps before your board of review, not to catch you out.

{{< drg/tip >}}
This requirement specifically comes after Scout rank requirement 7 — make sure that conference is complete before you schedule this one.
{{< /drg/tip >}}
