## Overview

Tenderfoot is where Scouting stops being talk and starts being practice. As a Scout, you learned the words — the Oath, the Law, the Outdoor Code. As a Tenderfoot, you put them to work: pitching a tent, tying a knot that holds real weight, treating a real scrape, testing your own fitness. This rank asks you to show what you can do, not just say what you know.

## Building on the Last Rank

You already carry the basics from Scout rank — the Oath and Law, the Outdoor Code, three basic knots, pocketknife safety. Tenderfoot doesn't retest those; it puts them to use in the field, on a real campout, under real conditions. Where a requirement here builds directly on something you covered as a Scout, this guide points back to that page instead of repeating it.

## Get Ready!

Grab your gear list, pick an upcoming campout, and start reading. Most of these requirements come alive on the trail, not on the couch — so timing your reading to an actual outing will make this rank easier to earn and easier to remember.
