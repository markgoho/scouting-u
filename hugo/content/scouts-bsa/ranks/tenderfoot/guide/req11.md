{{< drg/requirement number="11" text_format="html" >}}
Successfully complete your board of review for the Tenderfoot rank.
{{< /drg/requirement >}}

This is your first board of review — Scout rank didn't have one, so this format is new. It's the last step before your Tenderfoot rank is official.

## What a Board of Review Is

A board of review is a short meeting with a small panel of troop adults (usually two to four, not including your Scoutmaster). It is not a retest. The board isn't there to quiz you on knots or first aid again — that already happened during your work on each requirement and your Scoutmaster conference. Instead, they're checking that the process worked: that you completed the requirements, that your experience in the troop has been positive, and that you're ready to move forward.

## What to Expect

- You'll be asked to talk about your experience earning Tenderfoot — what you enjoyed, what was hard, what you learned.
- Questions are usually open-ended, not pass/fail trivia.
- The board wants an honest conversation, not rehearsed answers.

{{< drg/tip >}}
Dress in your full uniform and arrive a few minutes early. A board of review is a bit more formal than a Scoutmaster conference, and looking prepared sets a good tone.
{{< /drg/tip >}}

Once the board is satisfied, you'll be notified that you've earned Tenderfoot rank — the first of many boards of review ahead of you in Scouts BSA.
