Requirement 6c is the culmination of your 30-day fitness program. By retesting yourself across the same four disciplines under identical conditions, you evaluate how your body has adapted to consistent physical training.

## The Standardized Retest Protocol

To ensure your comparison is accurate and fair, you must replicate the exact testing conditions you used during Requirement 6a:

1. **Warm Up Properly**: Perform the same 5 to 10-minute dynamic warm-up and gentle stretching routine before beginning.
2. **Push-Ups (60 Seconds)**: Maintain strict form—straight back, elbows bending to a 90-degree right angle, pushing up to full arm extension. Count only full, correct repetitions completed in 1 minute.
3. **Sit-Ups or Curl-Ups (60 Seconds)**: Use the same variation chosen in 6a. For sit-ups, have a buddy hold your feet and ensure your elbows touch your thighs on every rep.
4. **Back-Saver Sit-and-Reach**: Use the same 12-inch box setup with the ruler's 9-inch mark aligned with the footplate. Measure both **Right** and **Left** legs after four smooth reaches, holding the fourth reach for 1 second.
5. **One-Mile Walk/Run**: Retest on the same 400-meter track or measured 1-mile route. Record total elapsed time in minutes and seconds.

## What "Improvement of Any Degree" Means

The requirement specifically states improvement **"of any degree."** This is not a competition against other Scouts or an arbitrary national percentile. It is a measure of your own dedication and personal growth:

- **Any positive gain counts**: Adding a single push-up, completing one more sit-up, reaching an extra quarter-inch on your hamstring stretch, or trimming two seconds off your mile run satisfies the requirement.
- **Why Your Body Improved**: Over 30 days of consistent exercise, your muscle fibers adapt to handle greater workloads, your cardiovascular system delivers oxygen to working muscles more efficiently, and your tendons and ligaments become more pliable.

## Discussing Your Results with Your Scoutmaster

During your Scoutmaster Conference, bring your 30-day activity log and your before-and-after comparison table. Be prepared to discuss:
- **What Worked Well**: Which exercises produced the most noticeable improvements, and how regular practice felt as the weeks went by.
- **Challenges Faced**: How you stayed motivated when tired or busy, and how you adjusted your routine around bad weather or school schedules.
- **Next Steps (Second Class Rank)**: Completing Tenderfoot 6c unlocks the next fitness challenge: **Second Class Requirement 7a**, where you will maintain an active lifestyle of at least 30 minutes a day, 5 days a week, for 4 weeks.

{{< drg/tip >}}
If an illness, minor injury, or pacing mistake prevented improvement on a specific test, do not panic or alter your numbers. Discuss what occurred honestly with your Scoutmaster. You can continue practicing that specific exercise for another week or two and retest.
{{< /drg/tip >}}
