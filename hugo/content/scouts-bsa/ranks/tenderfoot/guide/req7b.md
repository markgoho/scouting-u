{{< drg/requirement number="7b" text_format="html" >}}
Participate in a total of one hour of service in one or more service projects approved by your Scoutmaster. Explain how your service to others relates to the Scout slogan and Scout motto.
{{< /drg/requirement >}}

One hour doesn't sound like much, but it's your first formal service project as a Tenderfoot — and this requirement asks you to connect it back to two phrases you memorized as a Scout: the motto, "Be Prepared," and the slogan, "Do a Good Turn Daily."

## Planning Your Project

Talk to your Scoutmaster before you start — the requirement says "approved by your Scoutmaster," so get the project cleared first, not after the fact. Good options include:

- A troop-organized community service project (park cleanup, food drive, etc.).
- A school, faith community, or neighborhood service opportunity.
- Helping a neighbor with yard work, a move, or another concrete task.

## Connecting It to the Motto and Slogan

- **The slogan, "Do a Good Turn Daily,"** is about small, repeated acts of service — this hour is one deliberate example of that habit.
- **The motto, "Be Prepared,"** shows up in how you approached the project: did you bring the right tools, plan for the task, know what was expected of you?

Be ready to describe a specific moment from your service hour where one of these ideas actually applied — not just a general statement that service is good.
