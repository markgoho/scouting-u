This requirement covers the injuries and hazards you're most likely to actually run into outdoors — then asks you to go one step further and prevent them before they happen, and to be equipped when they do.

## What You'll Complete

- 4a. **[Show basic first aid](/scouts-bsa/ranks/tenderfoot/guide/req4a/)**: Show first aid for eight common outdoor injuries and conditions.
- 4b. **[Identify hazardous plants](/scouts-bsa/ranks/tenderfoot/guide/req4b/)**: Describe hazardous plants, identify any in your area, and explain treatment.
- 4c. **[Prevent outdoor injuries](/scouts-bsa/ranks/tenderfoot/guide/req4c/)**: Explain what prevents the injuries and exposures covered in 4a and 4b.
- 4d. **[Assemble first-aid kit](/scouts-bsa/ranks/tenderfoot/guide/req4d/)**: Build a personal first-aid kit and explain each item's use.

Work through these in order — prevention (4c) makes a lot more sense once you know what you're preventing (4a, 4b), and the kit you assemble in 4d should carry what you'd actually need for the injuries covered earlier in this requirement.
