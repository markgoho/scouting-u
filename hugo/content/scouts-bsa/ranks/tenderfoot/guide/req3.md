You already learned to tie the square knot, two half-hitches, and the taut-line hitch as a Scout. Tenderfoot doesn't ask you to relearn them — it asks you to *use* them, in a real situation where a knot that slips or fails actually matters. Requirement 3d adds an entirely new skill: caring for the knife, saw, and ax you'll rely on for the rest of your Scouting career.

## What You'll Complete

- 3a. **[Demonstrate square knot](/scouts-bsa/ranks/tenderfoot/guide/req3a/)**: Use the square knot for a real task, like joining two ropes or tying a bundle.
- 3b. **[Demonstrate two half-hitches](/scouts-bsa/ranks/tenderfoot/guide/req3b/)**: Use two half-hitches to secure a rope to something under tension.
- 3c. **[Demonstrate taut-line hitch](/scouts-bsa/ranks/tenderfoot/guide/req3c/)**: Use the taut-line hitch to guy out a tent or tarp.
- 3d. **[Care for knife, saw, and ax](/scouts-bsa/ranks/tenderfoot/guide/req3d/)**: Show proper care, sharpening, and use of the knife, saw, and ax.

The best way to complete 3a-3c is on the same campout you're using for requirements 1 and 2 — pitching a tarp, guying out a tent, or securing gear all give you a real "practical use" instead of a demonstration on flat ground.
