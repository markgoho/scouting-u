This requirement runs over at least 30 days, not one sitting: test yourself now, work on a plan, then test yourself again after tracking at least a month of activity.

## What You'll Complete

- 6a. **[Record fitness benchmark](/scouts-bsa/ranks/tenderfoot/guide/req6a/)**: Record your current results in four fitness tests.
- 6b. **[Develop improvement plan](/scouts-bsa/ranks/tenderfoot/guide/req6b/)**: Build a plan and track your activity against it for at least 30 days.
- 6c. **[Show fitness improvement](/scouts-bsa/ranks/tenderfoot/guide/req6c/)**: Retest yourself after at least 30 days and show improvement.

Keep your own written record of your baseline numbers and your daily or weekly activity — you'll need them once your 30 days (or more) are up to show real improvement in 6c.
