Citizenship at Tenderfoot covers two very different acts: showing respect for a national symbol, and putting in real time to serve your community.

## What You'll Complete

- 7a. **[Demonstrate American flag etiquette](/scouts-bsa/ranks/tenderfoot/guide/req7a/)**: Show how to display, raise, lower, and fold the American flag.
- 7b. **[Complete 1 hour of service](/scouts-bsa/ranks/tenderfoot/guide/req7b/)**: Complete one hour of approved service and connect it to the Scout motto and slogan.

Flag etiquette is a hands-on skill you can practice at a troop meeting; the service hour needs advance planning with your Scoutmaster, so don't leave it until the last minute.
