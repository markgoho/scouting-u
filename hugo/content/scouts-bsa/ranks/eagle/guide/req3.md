{{< drg/requirement number="3" text_format="html" >}}
Earn a total of 21 merit badges (10 more than required for the Life rank), including these 13 merit badges: (a) First Aid, (b) Citizenship in the Community, (c) Citizenship in the Nation, (d) Citizenship in the World, (e) Communication, (f) Cooking, (g) Personal Fitness, (h) Emergency Preparedness OR Lifesaving, (i) Environmental Science OR Sustainability, (j) Personal Management, (k) Swimming OR Hiking OR Cycling, (l) Camping, and (m) Family Life. <br><br> You must choose only one of the merit badges listed in categories h, i, and k. Any additional merit badge(s) earned in those categories may be counted as one of your eight optional merit badges used to make your total of 21.<br><br>
{{< /drg/requirement >}}

You came into Eagle with [11 merit badges from Life, 7 of them from the Eagle-required list](/scouts-bsa/ranks/life/guide/req3/). This requirement adds ten more to reach 21 total, and raises the required-list minimum from 7 to 13.

## The 13 Required Categories

Every one of these has to be covered by a merit badge you've earned:

- (a) First Aid
- (b) Citizenship in the Community
- (c) Citizenship in the Nation
- (d) Citizenship in the World
- (e) Communication
- (f) Cooking
- (g) Personal Fitness
- (h) Emergency Preparedness **or** Lifesaving
- (i) Environmental Science **or** Sustainability
- (j) Personal Management
- (k) Swimming **or** Hiking **or** Cycling
- (l) Camping
- (m) Family Life

Categories h, i, and k each give you a choice — you only need one badge from each pair or trio to satisfy that category. If you earn more than one badge from the same choice (both Emergency Preparedness and Lifesaving, say), the extra one doesn't count twice toward your 13 — it rolls into your total as one of the eight optional badges instead.

## Doing the Math

You already have 7 Eagle-required badges from Life, so you need at least 6 more from the 13-category list above to reach 13. That leaves 4 more badges of any kind — Eagle-required or not — to bring your total from 21 minus 13 required up to 21 overall.

- **10 more merit badges** total, to reach 21.
- **6 more** of those must come from categories on the 13-category list that you haven't covered yet.
- **4 more** can be any merit badge at all.

If you came into Eagle with more than 7 Eagle-required badges already — because you earned extras in categories h, i, or k, or picked up Eagle-required badges beyond Life's minimum — adjust the math: subtract your actual Eagle-required count from 13 to find how many required categories you still need to fill, and the rest of your ten can be optional.

{{< drg/tip >}}
Citizenship in Society is not on this list. If your troop or a merit badge counselor mentions it, double-check which version of the requirements you're working from — the 13 categories above are what this rank currently requires.
{{< /drg/tip >}}

{{< drg/checklist title="Tracking Your 21" subtitle="Work through this with your advancement records in hand" >}}

- Total badges earned: count toward 21.
- Eagle-required categories covered: check off each of the 13 letters above, using only one badge per h/i/k choice.
- Categories still open: subtract your covered count from 13 to see what's left.
- Optional badges: any badge beyond your 13 required counts here, including extras from h, i, or k.
