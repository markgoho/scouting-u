{{< drg/requirement number="1" text_format="html" >}}
Be active in your troop for at least six months as a Life Scout.
{{< /drg/requirement >}}

Requirement 1 establishes the final active tenure milestone: **at least six months of continuous active participation as a Life Scout**. 

---

## 1. Defining "Active Participation" Under the Guide to Advancement

According to the *Guide to Advancement* (topic 4.2.3.1), active participation is determined by three clear national standards:

1. **Official Registration**: You must be currently registered in your unit (troop, crew, or ship) and in good standing with Scouting America.
2. **Good Standing**: You adhere to the Scout Oath and Scout Law, unit policies, and safety rules.
3. **Meeting Unit Participation Expectations**: You attend unit meetings, outdoor activities, campouts, and service projects with reasonable consistency.

{{< drg/did-you-know >}}
The *Guide to Advancement* explicitly states that units cannot establish rigid mathematical attendance percentages (e.g., "must attend 80% of all meetings") as an absolute barrier to advancement. If academic commitments, sports seasons, or family responsibilities temporarily reduce your attendance, communicate with your Scoutmaster proactively to maintain agreed-upon active engagement.
{{< /drg/did-you-know >}}

---

## 2. Calculating the Six-Month Tenure Window

- **Start Date**: The clock starts on the date you successfully passed your **Life Scout Board of Review**.
- **Elapsed Time**: Exactly six calendar months must elapse before your Eagle Scout rank application can be certified. For example, if you earned Life on January 15, your six-month active tenure requirement is satisfied on July 15.
- **Concurrent Requirements**: While completing your six months of active tenure, you will work concurrently on your remaining merit badges ([Requirement 3](/scouts-bsa/ranks/eagle/guide/req3/)), your position of responsibility ([Requirement 4](/scouts-bsa/ranks/eagle/guide/req4/)), and your Eagle Scout Service Project ([Requirement 5](/scouts-bsa/ranks/eagle/guide/req5/)).

---

## 3. Leading as a Life Scout

During these six months, your role within the troop reaches its highest maturity:
- **Set the Standard**: Model punctuality, complete uniforming, outdoor ethics, and cheerful service.
- **Support Unit Leadership**: Work alongside your Senior Patrol Leader, mentoring newer patrol leaders and assisting with meeting planning and execution.
- **Foster Patrol Spirit**: Ensure your patrol functions effectively and that younger Scouts feel supported in their advancement.

{{< drg/checklist title="Active Life Scout Checklist" subtitle="Habits for your six-month Eagle tenure" >}}
- Maintain consistent attendance at troop meetings and monthly campouts.
- Serve actively and visibly in your approved leadership position.
- Mentor younger Scouts working toward Tenderfoot, Second Class, and First Class.
- Proactively communicate with adult leaders regarding project timelines and scheduling.
{{< /drg/checklist >}}

{{< drg/tip >}}
Verify the exact date of your Life board of review on your official Scouts BSA advancement profile in Scoutbook or Internet Advancement. This exact date must be recorded on line 1 of your Eagle Scout Rank Application.
{{< /drg/tip >}}
