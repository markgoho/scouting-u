{{< drg/requirement number="1" text_format="html" >}}
Be active in your troop for at least six months as a Life Scout.
{{< /drg/requirement >}}

Life asked for six months as an active Star Scout. Eagle asks for the same length of time again — six months — but now as an active Life Scout. Unlike the jump from Star to Life, the clock itself doesn't get longer here; six months is as long as this tenure requirement ever gets.

## What Counts as Active

"Active" means what it's meant at every rank since Star: showing up for your patrol and troop meetings and outings on a regular basis, and taking part when you're there. Missing an occasional meeting for a real reason — illness, a family trip, a school conflict — doesn't reset the clock. Your Scoutmaster is the one who confirms whether your six months counted as active, so raise a questionable absence with them early rather than waiting until your board of review.

## Starting the Clock

The six months start from the day you earned Life, not from whenever you get around to starting Eagle's other requirements. Your merit badges, your position of responsibility, and your service project all run on their own timelines, so there's no reason to wait on this clock before starting the rest.

{{< drg/tip >}}
This same six months is also what has to be behind you before your board of review in [requirement 7](/scouts-bsa/ranks/eagle/guide/req7/) can happen — it's one clock doing two jobs, not two separate waiting periods.
{{< /drg/tip >}}
