{{< /drg/requirement >}}

No earlier rank asked you to lead anyone. Star is the first rank that requires a real position of responsibility — an elected or appointed job with actual duties, held for four months while you're a First Class Scout.

## Choosing a Position

The list of approved positions depends on your unit type:

- **Scout troop**: patrol leader, assistant senior patrol leader, senior patrol leader, troop guide, Order of the Arrow troop representative, den chief, scribe, librarian, historian, quartermaster, bugler, junior assistant Scoutmaster, chaplain aide, instructor, webmaster, or outdoor ethics guide.
- **Venturing crew**: president, vice president, secretary, treasurer, den chief, historian, guide, quartermaster, chaplain aide, or outdoor ethics guide.
- **Sea Scout ship**: boatswain, boatswain's mate, purser, yeoman, storekeeper, crew leader, media specialist, specialist, den chief, or chaplain aide.
- **Lone Scout**: a leadership responsibility in your school, religious organization, club, or elsewhere in your community.

{{< drg/tip >}}
Assistant patrol leader is not an approved position for Star, Life, or Eagle, even though it's a real patrol job — talk to your Scoutmaster before assuming a position counts.
{{< /drg/tip >}}

## The Leadership Project Alternative

If no position is open when you need one, the requirement offers another path: a Scoutmaster-approved leadership project that helps the troop. This isn't a shortcut — it still has to run about as long and take as much real responsibility as holding one of the listed positions would. Talk to your Scoutmaster before you start, not after, so you both agree it qualifies.

## Serving Actively

Four months in the position isn't the same as being elected for four months. "Serve actively" means you're actually doing the job's duties — running patrol meetings as patrol leader, keeping records as scribe, maintaining troop gear as quartermaster — for the whole window, not just holding the title. This four-month window can run alongside the four months you're logging for [requirement 1](/scouts-bsa/ranks/star/guide/req1/); they don't have to happen one after the other.

{{< drg/tip >}}
Ask the Scout or adult currently in the role what the job actually involves before you run for it or accept it. A position that sounds simple on the list — scribe, librarian — often has more real work behind it than the title suggests.
{{< /drg/tip >}}
