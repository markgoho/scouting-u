{{< drg/requirement number="7" text_format="html" >}}
While a First Class Scout, participate in a Scoutmaster conference.
{{< /drg/requirement >}}

You've sat through three Scoutmaster conferences already — at [Tenderfoot](/scouts-bsa/ranks/tenderfoot/guide/req10/), [Second Class](/scouts-bsa/ranks/second-class/guide/req11/), and [First Class](/scouts-bsa/ranks/first-class/guide/req12/) — so the format is familiar: a one-on-one conversation with your Scoutmaster, not a test.

## What's Different at Star

This conference covers new ground the earlier ones didn't. Your Scoutmaster will likely ask how your position of responsibility is going, not just whether you hold one, and how your merit badge work toward Eagle is progressing. Star is also the first rank where your Scoutmaster is confirming a real span of time — four months as an active First Class Scout — rather than just a set of finished tasks.

## Getting Ready

- Be ready to talk about your position of responsibility: what you actually do in it, and what you've learned.
- Bring your notes on the merit badges you've started or finished toward your six.
- Think through your Scout spirit examples from [requirement 2](/scouts-bsa/ranks/star/guide/req2/) ahead of time.
- Have your service hours from [requirement 4](/scouts-bsa/ranks/star/guide/req4/) ready to describe, even if they're not finished yet.

{{< drg/tip >}}
This conversation goes better when you treat it as a check-in with someone who wants you to succeed, not an interrogation. If something isn't going well — a position that feels stalled, a merit badge that's stuck — say so. Your Scoutmaster can often help.
{{< /drg/tip >}}
