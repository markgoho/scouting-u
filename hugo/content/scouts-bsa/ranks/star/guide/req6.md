Work through each child requirement below in order. Use this page as your roadmap before you open the first detailed child page.

## What You'll Complete

- 6a. **[Complete child protection exercises](/scouts-bsa/ranks/star/guide/req6a/)**: Work through a parent-guardian pamphlet on recognizing and preventing child abuse, together with your parent or guardian.
- 6b. **[View Personal Safety Awareness videos](/scouts-bsa/ranks/star/guide/req6b/)**: Watch BSA's Personal Safety Awareness videos, with your parent or guardian's permission.

Both parts of this requirement need a parent or guardian involved, not just your Scoutmaster — plan to do them at home rather than at a troop meeting.
