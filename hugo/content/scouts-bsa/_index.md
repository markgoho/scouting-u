## The Scouting Method & Core Values

> "The patrol system is the one essential feature in which Scout training differs from that of all other organizations, and where the system is properly applied, it is bound to bring off any boy."  
> — _Robert Baden-Powell_

{{< drg/did-you-know >}}
**The Patrol Method in Action:**  
In Scouts BSA, patrols of 6–8 Scouts operate as small democratic teams. The elected Patrol Leader represents the patrol at the Patrol Leaders' Council (PLC), organizes camp duty rosters, and ensures every Scout participates in campouts and activities.
{{< /drg/did-you-know >}}

{{< drg/tip >}}
**Teaching Skills with the EDGE Method:**  
Whenever teaching an outdoor skill or requirement to newer Scouts, always use the 4-step framework:

- **Explain** — Describe what you are doing and why it is important.
- **Demonstrate** — Show the exact steps clearly at a slow pace.
- **Guide** — Coach the Scout as they practice the skill themselves.
- **Enable** — Step back and allow the Scout to perform the skill on real campouts and trail treks!
  {{< /drg/tip >}}

**The Outdoor Code:**  
As an American, I will do my best to:

- **Be clean in my outdoor manners.**
- **Be careful with fire.**
- **Be considerate in the outdoors.**
- **Be conservation-minded.**
