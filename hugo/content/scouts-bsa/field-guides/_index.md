Welcome to the **Scouts BSA Field Guides**. Whether you are a new Scout practicing for your next rank, a Patrol Leader organizing a campout, or getting ready for a big backpacking trek, these guides give you quick, practical answers in the field.

Each guide includes step-by-step instructions, clear diagrams, and helpful pro tips.

## Outdoor Guides

### 🪢 [Knots, Hitches & Lashings Field Guide](/scouts-bsa/field-guides/knots-and-lashings/)
Everything you need to know about ropework:
- **Core Knots**: Step-by-step instructions for the Square Knot, Bowline, Taut-line Hitch, Clove Hitch, Two Half-Hitches, and Sheet Bend.
- **Pioneering Lashings**: How to tie Square, Diagonal, Shear, Tripod, and Floor lashings for camp gadgets and towers.
- **Rope Care**: How to whip rope with cord or fuse synthetic ends with heat so they don't unravel.

[**Open the Knots & Lashings Guide &rarr;**](/scouts-bsa/field-guides/knots-and-lashings/)

### 🩹 [Wilderness First Aid Guide](/scouts-bsa/field-guides/wilderness-first-aid/)
How to handle outdoor injuries and medical emergencies:
- **Patient Assessment**: How to check an injured Scout quickly using the MARCH PAWS method.
- **Treating Injuries**: Managing bleeding, applying SAM splints to sprains and broken bones, and cleaning wounds.
- **Cold & Heat Emergencies**: How to build a hypothermia burrito wrap and treat heat exhaustion.
- **Calling for Help**: When to walk out, when to call Search & Rescue, and how to use satellite messengers or signal mirrors.

[**Open the Wilderness First Aid Guide &rarr;**](/scouts-bsa/field-guides/wilderness-first-aid/)

### 🍳 [Camp Cooking & Kitchen Guide](/scouts-bsa/field-guides/camp-cooking/)
Master your patrol kitchen and cook great camp meals:
- **The 3-Pot Wash System**: How to wash, rinse, and sanitize dishes in camp so nobody gets sick.
- **Dutch Oven Cooking**: How many charcoal briquettes to put on top and bottom for baking, simmering, and roasting.
- **Camp Stoves & Fuel**: How to safely use canister stoves and liquid fuel stoves in cold weather.
- **Food Safety & Bear Triangle**: Keeping coolers below 40°F and keeping food 200 feet away from your sleeping tents.

[**Open the Camp Cooking Guide &rarr;**](/scouts-bsa/field-guides/camp-cooking/)

### 🧭 [Navigation & Weather Guide](/scouts-bsa/field-guides/navigation-and-weather/)
Find your way on trails and read the weather:
- **Topographic Maps**: How to read contour lines to spot steep hills, ridges, and valleys.
- **Baseplate Compass**: How to take a field bearing, take a map bearing, and follow a bearing on the trail.
- **Declination**: Simple math rules to adjust between True North and Magnetic North.
- **Predicting Weather**: How to look at cloud shapes and wind direction to know if rain is coming.

## The 10 Outdoor Essentials

{{< drg/be-prepared title="The Outdoor Essentials for Every Outing" >}}
Always pack these 10 essentials in your daypack before every hike or campout:
1. **Navigation:** Topographic map and a baseplate compass.
2. **Headlamp / Flashlight:** LED headlamp with extra batteries.
3. **Sun Protection:** Sunscreen, sunglasses, and a hat.
4. **First Aid Kit:** Bandages, blister moleskin, antiseptic wipes, and gloves.
5. **Pocketknife:** A clean, sharp folding knife (with your Totin' Chip card).
6. **Fire Starters:** Waterproof matches and fire starter tinder.
7. **Emergency Shelter:** A light emergency space blanket or small tarp.
8. **Extra Food:** Trail mix, energy bars, or jerky for an extra day.
9. **Extra Water:** At least 2 water bottles plus a water filter or purification tablets.
10. **Extra Clothes:** Warm fleece or wool layers and a waterproof rain jacket (never wear cotton in cold/wet weather!).
